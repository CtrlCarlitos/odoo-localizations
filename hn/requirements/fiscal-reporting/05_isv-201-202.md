## 1. Purpose

This file defines the functional requirements for the two ISV declaration
surfaces of master-index cluster F5: (a) the **código 201** *Declaración
Jurada de Impuesto Sobre Ventas* (ISV sworn declaration, monthly
determinativa) — *Sección A* débito auto-computed on the 15%/18% bases with
informative-only sales lines (*exentas*, *exoneradas persona natural*
D. 119/2016, *exoneradas OCE*, FYDUCA transfers, exports split
Centroamérica / fuera-de-la-región); *Sección B* — the 30-casilla credit
map across mercado interno / FYDUCA / importaciones with the "no
utilizadas en plazo" lines (credits whose Art. 12 four-month window =
cause month + 3 has lapsed), the pro-rata trichotomy (100% gravada /
mixed % of gravadas sales / exentas → costo-o-gasto feeding DMC 270/280/
290) and the DMC→201 auto-fill categories 131/132/133; *Sección C* —
payments, indefinite *arrastre* (carryforward of favorable balances),
SAR-authorized compensation/cesión fields and the retention credits
auto-fed from the DMR/DJIMR and editable against *comprobantes de
retención* (three feeds: Estado/F-01, DEI-215-2010 Art. 8 activities,
OTCD cards 10%); *Sección D* liquidation; plus DMC-first sequencing
(5 días < 10 días, R-H36) and the hecho-generador period-assignment
reporting rules (invoice-first goods, earliest-of services with advance
trigger, *retiro*, import *nacionalización*) and the *bienes usados*
(used goods) exempt-resale carve-out; and (b) the **código 202**
*Régimen Simplificado* annual declaration — INFORMATIVE only per R-H23
(no payment, no collection duty, NO reduced rate anywhere), gates
(≤ L250,000 gravadas sales, single establishment, 2-year lookback),
31-ene deadline, ÷60×360 mid-year-start annualization and
reclasificación.

It does **not** cover: the law-side ISV semantics — rates, base
composition, débito/crédito eligibility, the 4-month window rule, arrastre
and RS gates as *legal* rules — owned by
[../taxation/06_isv.md](../taxation/06_isv.md) (HN-TAX-FR-211..255) and
consumed here BY ID, never re-derived; the OVI/SW filing chassis
(login→juramento→acuse→estado→rectificativa→Pagar/Boletín) — file 01
(cluster F1), consumed by id; the DMC 527 line contract, its 5-day
calendar dated rows and plantilla/SW validation — file 03 (cluster F3),
consumed by id; the DJIMR/DMR per-código retention engine whose third-party
data feeds 201 *Sección C* — file 02 (cluster F2), consumed by id; the
tarjetas 215/523 declaration surfaces — file 04 (cluster F4), consumed by
id; CT sanctions/procedure mechanics — taxation file 01 (T11 frame,
HN-TAX-FR-001..045); and the 8% card *devolución* (F4 / taxation/06
FR-255).

## 3. Functional Requirements

Numbering: HN-FREP-FR-151..187 (allocated range 151..190; contiguous).

### 3.1 Form 201: identity, channel, calendar, sequencing

- **HN-FREP-FR-151:** The system shall implement the *Declaración Jurada
  de Impuesto Sobre Ventas* — código **201**, tipo **Determinativa**,
  monthly — as the ISV determination surface for all responsables
  (sellers, service providers, importers/customs agents per Art. 8),
  filed through the OVI única modalidad per SAR-236-2024, with the
  full chassis behavior (juramento attestation, acuse with código
  único + QR, estado pill, same-modality rectificativa, Pagar/Boletín)
  consumed by id from fiscal-reporting file 01 (cluster F1), and the
  always-file duty (zero balance / crédito-favor / temporary closure)
  consumed by id from HN-TAX-FR-252. (LB-001; LB-010; LB-014;
  EV43:EVID-137; EV43:EVID-136)
- **HN-FREP-FR-152:** The system shall carry the 201 calendar as dated
  configuration rows: presentation AND payment of ISV "dentro de los
  primeros diez (10) días calendario del mes siguiente a aquel en que
  se efectuaron las ventas", with due-day semantics per instrument
  (calendario here; the manuals' hedge that the Administration may
  modify deadlines recorded as a dated-row re-verification trigger).
  (LB-001; LB-014; EV43:EVID-137; EV43:EVID-149)
- **HN-FREP-FR-153:** The system shall enforce DMC-first sequencing as a
  prerequisite gate on 201 submission: the informativa DMC (form 527,
  5 días calendario per SAR-237-2024 — deadline chain and contract owned
  by fiscal-reporting file 03, cluster F3, consumed by id) must be filed
  for the period before the 201 determinativa is presented, per the
  selection-time reminder "recuerde previamente es la informativa de
  Declaración Mensual de Compra (DMC)" and the 5d < 10d design (R-H36);
  the gate shall never auto-file the DMC. (LB-011; LB-014;
  EV43:EVID-137; EV43:EVID-144; R-H36)

### 3.2 Declaration-period assignment (hecho-generador reporting)

Law-side event semantics are consumed BY ID from taxation/06
(HN-TAX-FR-212..216); this file owns the period-assignment surface: the
event date — not the posting date — selects the 201 monthly period
(D-H2 event-first).

- **HN-FREP-FR-154:** The system shall assign each sale of goods to the
  monthly 201 period of its hecho-generador date — factura-emission date
  first, else the delivery date (even under reserva de dominio) —
  stamping the period on the declaration line, never deriving it from
  accounting posting dates. (LB-004; EV43:EVID-149; taxation/06
  HN-TAX-FR-213 by id)
- **HN-FREP-FR-155:** The system shall assign each service to the
  monthly 201 period of the EARLIEST of factura date, service-rendered
  date, or pago/abono (payment or advance) date — advances received
  trigger a débito in the period of receipt. (LB-004; EV43:EVID-149;
  taxation/06 HN-TAX-FR-214 by id)
- **HN-FREP-FR-156:** The system shall assign self-supply (*uso o
  consumo para uso propio*) to the period of "la fecha del retiro" and
  imports to the period of "la nacionalización del bien o de la
  liquidación y pago de la póliza correspondiente". (LB-004;
  EV43:EVID-149; taxation/06 HN-TAX-FR-212/215 by id)
- **HN-FREP-FR-157:** The system shall flag *bienes usados* (used
  goods) with an exempt-resale treatment on the reporting surface: ISV
  paid at import "constituirá un costo" (cost, no credit → costo/gasto
  bucket), and internal resale is NOT taxed ("no se gravarán") — resale
  revenue excluded from gravadas lines and reported on the informative
  exempt side; the Odoo exempt-resale flag design = OQ-005 (69_ OQ-2;
  law rule consumed from HN-TAX-FR-216 by id). (LB-004; EV43:EVID-149)

### 3.3 Sección A — débito and informative sales lines

- **HN-FREP-FR-158:** The system shall auto-compute the *débito fiscal*
  on completing the Sección A rate bases — "el Débito fiscal será
  determinado de forma automática por el sistema al completar cualquiera
  de las casillas anteriores" — as base × rate on the two gravadas
  baskets (Ventas en el mercado Interno al 15% / al 18%), net of taxes
  returned for *ventas anuladas o rescindidas* and for rebajas/descuentos
  of the period (rates and débito law semantics = HN-TAX-FR-223/236 by
  id), closing with the "Total Débito del Período" footer.
  (LB-002; LB-003; LB-014; EV43:EVID-138; EV43:EVID-148)
- **HN-FREP-FR-159:** The system shall report *Ventas exentas en el
  mercado interno* as an informative-only line that never generates
  débito ("no generan Débito fiscal, sin embargo, de existir ventas bajo
  estos conceptos deberán ser informadas"). (LB-014; EV43:EVID-138)
- **HN-FREP-FR-160:** The system shall report *Ventas exoneradas persona
  natural (Decreto 119/2016) (Gravadas al 15%)* as a named
  informative-only line preserving the print's hybrid label verbatim
  (exoneradas yet "Gravadas al 15%") with NO débito effect until the
  decreto's mechanics are pinned (LEAD — OQ-002); the system shall never
  silently resolve the hybrid. (LB-007; LB-014; EV43:EVID-138)
- **HN-FREP-FR-161:** The system shall report *Ventas exoneradas OCE* as
  an informative-only line with no débito (OCE purchase-order mechanics =
  HN-TAX-FR-246 by id; the exoneradas-OCE purchase mirror = FR-170).
  (LB-014; EV43:EVID-138)
- **HN-FREP-FR-162:** The system shall report *Transferencias de bienes
  y servicios FYDUCA Gravadas al 15% y al 18%* as informative-only sales
  lines with no débito. (LB-014; EV43:EVID-138)
- **HN-FREP-FR-163:** The system shall report exports on two separate
  informative baskets — *Exportaciones (Fuera de la región
  Centroamericana)* and *Exportaciones Región centroamericana* — neither
  generating débito (tasa cero law-side = HN-TAX-FR-226 by id; the
  CA/fuera-CA split mirrored on the import side, FR-168).
  (LB-002; LB-014; EV43:EVID-138; EV43:EVID-148)

### 3.4 Sección B — the 30-casilla credit map

- **HN-FREP-FR-164:** The system shall implement Sección B
  "Determinación del Impuesto Compras" as the 30-line credit map with
  three origins — mercado interno (lines 1-8), FYDUCA (11-16),
  importaciones (17-30) — each carrying 15%/18% base lines, auto credit
  lines and non-credit informative lines, closing with "Total de
  créditos declarados en las compras"; casilla NAMES are the contract of
  record (numbered template = OQ-001). (LB-014; EV43:EVID-139)
- **HN-FREP-FR-165:** The system shall auto-compute credit lines as
  base × rate per origin/rate basket and shall key the entry mode on DMC
  obligation: "Esta sección vendrá autocompletada para los Obligados
  Tributarios (OT) que tengan obligación de presentar la Declaración
  Mensual de Compras (DMC). Para los OT que no tengan la obligación de
  presentar DMC los campos estarán habilitados para ingresar los montos
  correspondientes"; once a 201 is presented its Sección B snapshot
  freezes (D-H2.5 filed-period protection — later DMC changes require a
  201 rectificativa, never silent mutation). (LB-003; LB-014;
  EV43:EVID-139; taxation/06 HN-TAX-FR-237 by id)
- **HN-FREP-FR-166:** The system shall apply the pro-rata trichotomy
  when feeding Sección B from purchases: gravada-linked credit 100%;
  unidentifiable credit at "el porcentaje correspondiente a las ventas
  gravadas del período"; exentas-linked credit denied and routed to
  costo/gasto — emitting the DMC 270 (Monto al costo) / 280 (Monto al
  gasto) / 290 (Valor no deducible) split per the file-03 line contract
  by id (denominator refinement = OQ-013; law rule = HN-TAX-FR-243 by
  id). (LB-003; LB-017; EV43:EVID-148; EV43:EVID-150)
- **HN-FREP-FR-167:** The system shall implement the "no utilizadas en
  plazo" lines per rate basket and origin: purchase credits "cuyo crédito
  fiscal ha superado el período para ser utilizado conforme al artículo
  12" — i.e. whose four-month window (cause month + the three
  immediately following monthly periods, keyed to the DMC *fecha
  contable* per the file-03 contract by id) has lapsed — move to the
  dedicated base lines (*Compras gravadas no utilizadas en plazo 15%* /
  18% and the import mirrors incl. the fuera-CA variants) with the
  system auto-computing the corresponding credit "de forma informativa"
  (window law = HN-TAX-FR-242 by id). (LB-003; LB-014; EV43:EVID-139;
  EV43:EVID-148)
- **HN-FREP-FR-168:** The system shall feed the import baskets' CA vs.
  fuera-de-la-región split (Importaciones al 15%/18% and their "no
  utilizadas en plazo" and fuera-CA variants) from the DMC 527-54 split
  bases 1513/1520 (15%) and 1613/1620 (18%) per the file-03 line
  contract, consumed by id. (LB-014; EV43:EVID-139; EV43:EVID-146)
- **HN-FREP-FR-169:** The system shall never generate crédito fiscal
  from the exentas / exoneradas / OCE purchase lines of any origin —
  "tanto las compras exentas y exoneradas en el mercado interno no
  generan crédito fiscal" — reporting them informative-only
  (incl. *Adquisiciones FYDUCA exoneradas con OCE* and *Importaciones
  exentas / exoneradas con OCE*). (LB-003; LB-014; EV43:EVID-139)
- **HN-FREP-FR-170:** The system shall auto-fill Sección B for Grandes y
  Medianos Contribuyentes from DMC data in the three categories
  "• 131 – Compras en el mercado interno • 132 – FYDUCA • 133 –

  the 131/132/133 codes surface only in 70_ and must be reconciled

  (LB-017; EV43:EVID-150)

### 3.5 Sección C — special credits and retention credits

- **HN-FREP-FR-171:** The system shall auto-fill *Pagos realizados en el
  período* only when a paid value exists "para este impuesto y período".
  (LB-014; EV43:EVID-140)
- **HN-FREP-FR-172:** The system shall auto-fill *Excedentes del período
  anterior* from the prior 201's favorable balance and carry any
  credit-favor saldo forward month to month "hasta agotarlo"
  (indefinite arrastre; law rule = HN-TAX-FR-244 by id), leaving the
  field visibly empty-with-trace when the prior month produced none.
  (LB-003; LB-014; LB-016; EV43:EVID-140; EV43:EVID-148)
- **HN-FREP-FR-173:** The system shall gate *Importe a compensar* and
  *Cesiones de crédito* fields on prior SAR authorization — "se
  utilizará solo cuando se tengan créditos a compensar autorizados por
  el Servicio de Administración de Rentas (SAR)" — requiring the
  authorization reference (cession mechanics kin CT Art. 142, T11 frame
  by id). (LB-014; EV43:EVID-140)
- **HN-FREP-FR-174:** The system shall auto-fill the *Impuestos
  retenidos* credit fields from retentions reported by third parties via
  the DMR/DJIMR (file 02, cluster F2, consumed by id) AND shall allow
  the taxpayer to modify these values "al comprobar que los mismos
  difieren de los valores sustentados mediante los comprobantes de
  retención proporcionados por los agentes de retención", recording the
  comprobante-backed override with a full audit trail and the
  reconcile-first guidance ("Asegúrese de tener su comprobante de
  retención y que sus créditos fiscales coincidan con la información
  reportada ante el SAR por su agente retenedor"). (LB-014; LB-017;
  EV43:EVID-140; EV43:EVID-150)
- **HN-FREP-FR-175:** The system shall model the three retention-credit
  feeds with per-feed comprobante semantics: (a) *Impuesto Retenido por
  Ventas al Estado* (D.L. 51-2003 + Budget Law designation; for CUT

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given Sección A entries of L100,000 at 15% and L50,000 at
  18%, then débito = L15,000 + L9,000 = L24,000 auto-computed with no
  manual débito field; given an anulada credit note of L1,500 tax on a
  15% sale, then débito = L22,500 (FR-158).

  sales of L20,000 "(Gravadas al 15%)", exoneradas-OCE sales of L10,000
  and FYDUCA transfers of L5,000, then all four report informatively and
  débito stays L24,000 (FR-159..FR-162).
- **AC-003:** Given exports of L40,000 (Centroamérica) and L60,000
  (fuera de la región), then two separate informative baskets print and
  débito is unchanged (FR-163).
- **AC-004:** Given a purchase credit causated in January (fecha
  contable January) first applied in May, then the base moves to
  "Compras gravadas no utilizadas en plazo 15%" with an informative
  auto-credit, and it never reduces the payable (FR-167).
- **AC-005:** Given a DMC-obligado (grande contribuyente), then Sección
  B arrives auto-filled in categories 131/132/133 and manual re-keying is
  surface-logged; given a non-DMC obligado, then the same fields are
  manually enterable (FR-165, FR-170).

  then the 201 import 15% basket splits CA L10,000 / fuera-CA L4,000
  (FR-168).

  L100 of unidentifiable credit, then L60 enters crédito and L40 routes
  to the 270/280 costo/gasto buckets — never credit (FR-166).

  taxpayer's sales while the F-01-paid comprobantes sum L1,200, then the
  retention credit line opens at L1,000, is overridden to L1,200 with

  *Excedentes del período anterior* auto-fills L5,000; given the current
  month's credit-favor of L2,000, then next month auto-fills L2,000
  (indefinite arrastre) (FR-172).

  available credits L5,000 (payments + excedentes + retentions), then
  the liquidación shows payable L7,000 due within the first 10 días
  calendario of M+1 via Pagar/Boletín (FR-176, FR-152).

  filed 527 for P, then submission is blocked with the DMC-first message
  and no auto-filing occurs (FR-153).

  10-abr, then April (FR-154).

  L100,000, then the import ISV posts to cost (no credit) and the resale
  reports on the informative exempt side with the exempt-resale flag
  (FR-157).
- **AC-014:** Given a single-establishment retailer with gravadas
  L250,000 + exentas L90,000, then the RS gates pass (exentas excluded
  from the base); given gravadas L250,001 or a second establishment,
  then ordinary monthly 201 rules apply (FR-179).
