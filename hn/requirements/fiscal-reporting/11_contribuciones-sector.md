## 1. Purpose

This file owns the DECLARATION surfaces of master-index cluster F11 — the
seven SAR OVI *declaraciones juradas* (sworn declarations) of the
sector-contribution family, all **self-declared** (*Determinativa*, the
obliged party declares and pays itself — none is a
retention-at-source regime): the **monthly gross-income trio** of the Ley de
Seguridad Poblacional (LSP, Population-Security Law, D. 105-2011) family —
**código 502** telefonía móvil 1%, **503** comidas y bebidas bajo franquicias
0.5% (permanente since D. 31-2018) and **504** casinos y máquinas
tragamonedas 1% (license-gated); and the **annual net-excess quartet** —
**506** contribución especial sector cooperativo 3.6% (2011-2012 LSP family),
**509** contribución social sector cooperativo 15% + 0.5%-of-gross loss-year
floor (D. 53-2015/D. 92-2015, CONSUCOOP-recognized coops, ISR/IAN/Aportación
exempt since FY2014), **511** contribución social del sector social de la
economía 15% (D. 131-2018, the non-CONSUCOOP social economy) and **107**
contribución por excedente de operaciones de instituciones educativas 10%
(D. 17-2010 Art. 6). It owns the family's fiscal-calendar rows (monthly
first-10-days trio per ruling R-H28; the 506/509 Mar-30 and 511 close+3m
annual rows), the 509 loss-floor and *fuerza mayor* waiver mechanics
(May-31 auditor-firm report for savings-and-credit coops), the R-H34
glossary guard, and the 506-vs-509 boundary gate (BLOCKED on statute texts).

rectificativa frame, D-H2.5 freeze machinery) — fiscal-reporting file 01 of

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given a licensed casino operator with July-2026 gaming
  income, then a código 504 obligation renders for period 2026-07 with the
  two-section form and a due row inside the first 10 days of August 2026
  (calendario flagged-assumption); given an unlicensed operator, then no
  504 obligation is generated (FR-361, FR-363, FR-367, FR-374).
- **AC-002:** Given the trio manuals' "anualmente" Declaración-en-Plazos
  boilerplate, then the calendar for 502/503/504 renders MONTHLY rows —
  no annual row exists for any trio código (R-H28) (FR-365).
- **AC-003:** Given July gross telephony revenue L10,000,000, then the
  502 determination = 1% = L100,000.00; the obligation carries the
  transitorio-status flag, not a retirement (FR-370, FR-371).
- **AC-004:** Given a food/beverage PJ under an international franchise
  operating in a special regime with monthly gross L5,000,000, then 503 =
  0.5% = L25,000.00; given the same operator outside any special regime
  (or under a domestic franchise), then no 503 duty arises (FR-372,
  FR-373).
- **AC-005:** Given a period before D. 31-2018's vigencia with no pre-2018
  503 row loaded, then the 503 determination blocks with a config-gap flag
  — never the 0.5% row projected backwards (FR-373, FR-393).
- **AC-006:** Given 506 filing with annual cooperative net excess
  (ingresos − gastos) L1,000,000, then the determination = 3.6% =
  L36,000.00 computed once on the net excess (no second gastos
  subtraction from the form wording); due row = Mar-30 of the following
  year (FR-375, FR-376).
- **AC-007:** Given a CONSUCOOP-recognized cooperative with prior-FY
  ingresos totales L10M, gastos ordinarios/necesarios L6M and
  reservas/fondos/donaciones L1M, then the 509 base = L3M and the
  determination = 15% = L450,000.00; the partner carries the
  FY2014+ ISR/IAN/Aportación exemption flags (FR-377, FR-378).
- **AC-008:** Given a loss-year coop with declared gross income (total −
  discounts/rebajas/returns) L8M, then the 509 floor = 0.5% = L40,000.00
  on the legal definition — never on the form's excedente-stage
  "Ingresos Brutos" casilla (FR-379).
- **AC-009:** Given a savings-and-credit coop invoking fuerza mayor, then
  the waiver record requires the audit-firm financial report dated by
  May-31 and stores the scope flag (floor-removal vs
  accreditation-only) unresolved (FR-380).
- **AC-010:** Given the FY2026 509 declaration, then the operative due
  row = 2027-03-30 with the statute-vs-Rec conflict flag attached — not
  2027-03-31 (FR-381).
- **AC-011:** Given a cooperative partner with no explicit 506/509 regime
  selection, then neither obligation auto-generates and the selector
  surfaces the blocked boundary question; a manual selection of 506 does
  not silently open 509 (FR-382).
- **AC-012:** Given a social-economy PJ excluded from D. 53-2015/92-2015
  with ingresos L10M, gastos L5M, fondos (catalogued) L2M, then the 511
  Utilidad = L3M and the determination = 15% = L450,000.00; due row =
  2027-03-31 flagged derived (FR-383..FR-385).
- **AC-013:** Given a private university NOT under ISR Art. 22 with
  operating surplus L2,000,000, then 107 = 10% = L200,000.00 with the
  base consumed by id from taxation/05; given an institution under the
  Art. 22 quarterly-cuotas regime, then no 107 duty (FR-386, FR-387).
- **AC-014:** Given a presented 107 whose excedente differs from the
  same-period DJISR, then a reconciliation warning links the two records
  and no blocking occurs (FR-388).
- **AC-015:** Given the FY2026 annual close, then the 506 and 107
  declaration states feed file 07's FR-263 Apr-30 package roll-up by id
  while 509/511 sit on their own Mar-30/31 rows — 509/511 never appear
  in the Apr-30 package (FR-385, FR-390).
- **AC-016:** Given the 57_/58_ glossary EXCEDENTES entries, then no
  base definition of 502/503 loads from a glossary — the trio bases come
  from the §1/§3 statute quotes (R-H34) (FR-392).
- **AC-017:** Given a presented January 504 declaration later re-seeded
  with a statute-era rate row, then the presented record keeps its
  snapshot (código único + QR intact) and only future periods use the new
  row (FR-361, FR-393).
- **AC-018:** Given a 504 computation, then no per-machine amount or
  machine count from the municipal L-Art 22 levy enters the base, and no
  504 gross amount flows into any municipal surface (FR-391).
