## 1. Purpose

This file defines the functional requirements for the Decreto 17-2010 family
(*Ley de Fortalecimiento de los Ingresos, Equidad Social y Racionalización
del Gasto Público*, La Gaceta 32,193 of 22-abr-2010, vigencia 21-may-2010)
plus its reglamento (Acuerdo 1121-2010, `21_`) — clusters **T7** and **T10**.
It owns: (a) the **renta-de-capital cédulas and asset taxes** (T7) — the
dividend 10% *retención única y definitiva* (single-and-definitive retention)
by the sociedad, triggered at the earliest of agreement by the órgano
competente / payment / credit / being put at disposal, with in-kind
distributions at market value and retained amounts excluded from the Art. 22
progressive base; the deemed-dividend detectors (related-party receivables
>100 días calendario not arising from commercial operations — post-vigencia
creations only — and the capital-reduction waterfall *hasta agotar la
cuantía*); the holding-company / no-double-retention *cuenta separada* ledger
and the SEFIN-registry holding regime; the *cédular alquiler* 10% on gross
housing rents > L15,000/month aggregated across all units of the same
property (m² proration, mixed-ownership split, agent catalog); the
*enseñanza privada* 10% on operating surpluses; the elective asset
*revaluación* 6% *pago único y definitivo* with the *Superávit de Capital por
Revaluación de Activos* reserve account; and the *aportación solidaria
temporal* 5+5 schedule as HISTORICAL dead text; and (b) the
**selective/specific consumption taxes** (T10) — the vehicle *selectivo*
bands (new 10-60% / used 10-60% on base CIF + derechos arancelarios), the
*Ecotasa* L5,000/7,000/10,000 one-time charge expressly OUTSIDE the ISV base,
import-age prohibitions; cigarettes L350/millar with the IPC-adjustment
mechanism capped 6%/year; the beverage per-liter 2010 base table (gaseosa
L0.58 … licores L27.92, ron/aguardiente by proof degree) as dated base rows
whose CURRENT values' home is the W2 selectivo IPC acuerdos; slots
L25,000/machine/year (municipal) + the 5% electronic-lottery specific tax;
telecom bundle-splitting and prepaid mechanics; air 18% + the

Art. 3); and the D. 105/199-2011 permanent *contribuciones* closure (R-H11).

It does **not** cover: the current IPC-adjusted selectivo values — dated

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Dividend 10% retención única y definitiva (T7)

- **HN-TAX-FR-166:** The system shall effect the 10% retention on dividends
  or any participation in profits/reserves paid, credited, agreed or made
  available by a sociedad mercantil (the retention agent) to resident
  natural persons and to naturales who are partners of special-regime
  societies — the retention is *única y definitiva*: the retained dividend
  is flagged *definitive* and excluded from the recipient's Art. 22
  progressive scale base. (LB-001; LB-002; EV01:EVID-012; EV04:EVID-040)
- **HN-TAX-FR-167:** The system shall stamp the retention trigger at the
  EARLIEST of the dividend being *acordadas por el órgano competente*
  (agreed by the competent organ), paid, credited, or put at the disposal of
  the recipient — trigger date snapshotted on the distribution record (D15;
  D-H2 hecho-generador resolution). (LB-001; LB-002; EV01:EVID-012;
  EV04:EVID-040)
- **HN-TAX-FR-168:** The system shall compute the retention base of in-kind
  distributions (*en especie*) at the MARKET VALUE of the assets
  distributed. (LB-002; EV04:EVID-040)
- **HN-TAX-FR-169:** The system shall treat the capitalization of reserves
  or utilities as EXEMPT — "queda exento del pago de cualquier impuesto" —
  when approved by the competent organ and inscribed in the Libro de Actas
  (R-Art 6 formality), recording capitalization entries in the dividend
  ledger (feeding the FR-173 waterfall pools) with no retention. (LB-001;
  LB-002; EV01:EVID-012; EV04:EVID-040)
- **HN-TAX-FR-170:** The system shall route the dividend retention to the
  general retention calendar: monthly liquidation and entero at the latest
  within the first 10 días CALENDARIO of the following month, deposited
  with the TGR/SEFIN-authorized institutions (Arts. 26/34 anchor consumed
  from `01_isr-framework.md` HN-TAX-FR-031/032 and `04_isr-withholding.md`
  HN-TAX-FR-150 by cross-reference), emitting per-retention records for
  DJIMR código 113 (export contract = S-HN3). (LB-001; EV01:EVID-012/013)
- **HN-TAX-FR-171:** The system shall record the DAR supersession as a
  never-implement note: the reglamento's *Declaración Anual de Retenciones
  (DAR)* vehicle (R-Art 5) is HISTORICAL — per R-H31 the declaration
  surface is the monthly DJIMR/DMR system (código 113), with the dated
  regime rows DMR pre-2024 / DJIMR post-May-2024 owned by S-HN3; no DAR
  engine shall be implemented (resolved-by-supersession, §7 OQ-002).
  (LB-002; EV04:EVID-040; R-H31)

### 3.2 Deemed dividends (T7)

- **HN-TAX-FR-172:** The system shall implement the >100-day related-party
  receivable detector: receivables from socios or empresas relacionadas not
  arising from a commercial operation and with a term exceeding 100 días
  calendario are DEEMED DIVIDENDS subject to the FR-166 retention; the
  detector fires only on receivables CREATED after the D. 17-2010 vigencia
  (21-may-2010 — R-Art 4 cutoff, a dated filter), and receivables of
  lending companies (whose operations ARE commercial lending, R-Art 1.a)
  are excluded via a commercial-operation classification flag. (LB-001;
  LB-002; EV01:EVID-012; EV04:EVID-040)
- **HN-TAX-FR-173:** The system shall implement the capital-reduction
  waterfall: on a capital reduction with distribution, the FIRST amounts
  paid are deemed distributions of previously capitalized reserves/utilities
  "hasta agotar la cuantía" and bear the 10% retention; only the remainder
  passes as non-taxable corpus — pool tracing from the ledger's
  capitalization events (FR-169; the 2010 declared-but-unpaid transition
  rule is historical, no engine). (LB-002; EV04:EVID-040; EV01:EVID-012)

### 3.3 Holding-company no-double-retention ledger (T7)

- **HN-TAX-FR-174:** The system shall keep, per persona jurídica, the
  *cuenta separada* (separate account) of all dividend amounts RECEIVED
  with indication of their origin (*con indicación del origen*) — the
  dividend-received ledger distinguishing the already-retained (taxed) pool
  from any untaxed pool. (LB-001; EV01:EVID-012)
- **HN-TAX-FR-175:** The system shall suppress the FR-166 retention on
  downstream distributions traceable to the already-taxed pool of the
  FR-174 ledger (no double retention on dividends that bore the 10%
  upstream); distributions exceeding the taxed pool retain 10% on the
  untaxed portion. (LB-001; EV01:EVID-012)
- **HN-TAX-FR-176:** The system shall implement the holding-company regime:
  a persona jurídica whose sole activity is the ownership of shares, real
  management-direction of the held companies and participation in each of
  them (three-part statutory test as company flags) redistributes dividends
  from the held companies WITHOUT re-taxation; the SEFIN registry/alignment
  duty (within 30 days) is a compliance-status field. (LB-001;
  EV01:EVID-012)

### 3.4 Cédular alquiler de viviendas 10% (T7)

- **HN-TAX-FR-177:** The system shall compute the *cédula* on housing rents
  as an annual specific, single-and-definitive 10% tax on the gross rental
  price of renting housing/apartment buildings — base = gross rent "sin
  aplicar deducciones" (no deductions) — applying ONLY when the monthly
  rent EXCEEDS L15,000 computed as the total paid across ALL dwelling units
  or rooms of the SAME property (*inmueble*), never per unit. (LB-003;
  EV04:EVID-042)
- **HN-TAX-FR-178:** The system shall apply the mixed-lease proration: for
  a lease mixing dwelling and commercial use, itemized-and-realistic
  contract splits govern; otherwise the taxable dwelling portion = global
  rent × (dwelling m² ÷ total m²); the L15,000 threshold of FR-177 tests
  ONLY the dwelling portion. (LB-003; EV04:EVID-042)
- **HN-TAX-FR-179:** The system shall implement the subject rules:
  landlords subject to the cédula are NATURAL persons; co-owners of a
  property are a SINGLE taxable subject with one representative (DEI
  designates if absent); under mixed natural+juridical ownership income
  splits proportionally — the natural part pays the 10% cédula on its share
  while the L15,000 threshold tests the FULL property rent (not per-part).
  (LB-003; EV04:EVID-042)
- **HN-TAX-FR-180:** The system shall exclude from the cédula rents of
  hotels/motels/hospodajes (ISR ordinary rules) and pensiones/mesones/
  cuarterías governed by the Ley de Inquilinato D. 50-1979 (flag-only
  classification, mechanics outside this file). (LB-003; EV04:EVID-042)
- **HN-TAX-FR-181:** The system shall implement the agent-of-retention
  catalog: trust administrators over rental property, the owner or
  designated administrator, or the mandatario — retaining the 10% at
  collection with monthly entero (código 106/136 records, export contract
  = S-HN3; the 136 Tarifa value presumed 10% but unstated in the Ayuda =
  config, §7 OQ-008); where no retention occurred, the system flags the
  landlord's ISR Art. 28 annual-declaration procedure (R-Art 12 path).
  (LB-003; EV04:EVID-042)

### 3.5 Enseñanza privada 10% on surpluses (T7)

- **HN-TAX-FR-182:** The system shall compute the annual 10%
  *contribución* on the operating surplus (*excedente*) of private
  universities/schools/institutes (including NGO-operated centers):
  excedente = ordinary own-giro income + accessory income (EXCLUDING
  extraordinary) − expenses per ISR Arts. 11-12 rules, with
  scholarship-program expenses and aid to public schools deductible.
  (LB-004; EV04:EVID-043)
- **HN-TAX-FR-183:** The system shall suppress the FR-182 contribución for
  education entities subject to the *cuotas trimestrales* (pagos-a-cuenta

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given a sociedad agreeing on 15-mar-2026 a L100,000 dividend
  to a resident natural-person partner (payment later), then the retention
  stamps at the agreement date: 10% × 100,000 = L10,000.00, definitive and
  excluded from the recipient's progressive base (FR-166, FR-167).
- **AC-002:** Given an in-kind distribution of equipment with market value
  L50,000, then the retention = 10% × 50,000 = L5,000.00 on the
  market-value base (FR-168).
- **AC-003:** Given a L80,000 related-enterprise receivable created 2024,
  non-commercial, 120-day term, then a deemed dividend fires with 10% ×
  80,000 = L8,000.00; given an identical receivable created 01-may-2010
  (pre-vigencia), then no deemed dividend (FR-172).
- **AC-004:** Given a capital reduction distributing L200,000 where the
  ledger's capitalized-reserves pool holds L120,000, then the retention =
  10% × 120,000 = L12,000.00 and the remaining L80,000 passes as corpus
  with no retention (FR-173).
- **AC-005:** Given a holding company (three statutory tests met) receiving
  L50,000 of already-taxed dividends and redistributing them, then no
  second 10% retention and the ledger traces the taxed origin; given a
  non-holding PJ distributing L30,000 of which L10,000 traces to its taxed
  pool, then the retention = 10% × 20,000 = L2,000.00 on the untaxed
  portion only (FR-174..FR-176).
- **AC-006:** Given one property with three dwelling units renting L6,000 +
  L5,000 + L5,500 monthly, then the aggregate L16,500 > L15,000 triggers
  the cédula: 10% × 16,500 = L1,650.00 on the gross aggregate (no per-unit
  test) (FR-177).
- **AC-007:** Given a mixed global lease of L36,000/month with dwelling 200
  m² of 400 m² total and no realistic itemization, then the dwelling
  portion = 36,000 × (200/400) = L18,000.00 > L15,000 → cédula = 10% ×
  18,000 = L1,800.00 (the commercial L18,000 outside the cédula); given an
  itemized realistic split L18,000/L18,000, then the same result via the
  contract split (FR-178).
- **AC-008:** Given a property with full monthly rent L40,000 under 60%
  natural / 40% juridical ownership, then the cédula = 10% × 24,000 =
  L2,400.00 on the natural share while the threshold tests the full
  L40,000 (never the L24,000 per-part) (FR-179).
- **AC-009:** Given a private school with own-giro income L10,000,000 +
  accessory L500,000 − expenses L9,000,000 − scholarships L300,000 −
