## EVID-001 Ley ISR — hecho generador, concepto de ingreso y sujeto (Arts. 1-2, 6)

- **Loc:** Art. 1 (p.3), Art. 2 (pp.3-4), Art. 6 (p.6).
- **Verbatim:** "Se establece un impuesto anual denominado Impuesto sobre la Renta, que grava los ingresos provenientes del capital, del trabajo o de la combinación de ambos" (Art. 1). Ingreso = "cualquier percepción en efectivo, en valores, en especie o en crédito, que modifique el patrimonio del contribuyente" (Art. 1, p.3). Subjects: "Toda persona domiciliada o residente en Honduras, sea natural o jurídica, nacional o extranjera, inclusive la que goce de una concesión del Estado" — worldwide income: "ya sea que la fuente de la renta o ingreso esté situada dentro del país o fuera de él" (Art. 2, p.3). Merchant ships flagged/operating in Honduras (Art. 2.a-b). Herencias indivisas and goods administered "en virtud de un cargo" (Art. 6).
- **Gloss:** annual tax; residents taxed on worldwide income; income = patrimony-modifying perceptions (cash/kind/credit).
- **Candidate CRs:** residency-based tax-scope flag (worldwide vs source); estate/administered-goods taxpayer category.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-002 Ley ISR — fuente hondureña, exportaciones y vinculados (Art. 3)

- **Loc:** Art. 3 (pp.4-5).
- **Verbatim:** export income of goods produced/manufactured/treated/bought in-country is "totalmente de fuente hondureña"; export gross value = destination wholesale price ("deduciendo del precio de venta mayorista en el lugar de destino, el costo de tales bienes, los gastos de transporte y seguro..."). If no price or declared price below destination wholesale: "se considerará, salvo prueba en contrario, que existe vinculación económica entre el exportador del país y el importador del exterior" → the destination wholesale price governs (p.4). Mirror rule for imports (difference = Honduran-source income, "de cuyo impuesto ambos son solidariamente responsables"). Fallback comparable: "los coeficientes de resultados obtenidos por empresas independientes que se dedican a idéntica o similar actividad"; else AT sets "el por ciento neto" by analogy (p.5).
- **Gloss:** 1963-vintage transfer-pricing/related-party valuation rules (vinculación económica presumption + comparables + profit-coefficient fallback) — statutory base that coexists with the modern TP regime (Acuerdo 027-2015, later corpus addition).
- **Candidate CRs:** related-party export/import valuation guard (presumption trigger, wholesale-price basis, comparables fallback); solidary liability register.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-003 Ley ISR — non-resident gross-income withholding table (Arts. 4-5)

- **Loc:** Art. 4 (pp.4-5), Art. 5 (pp.5-6).
- **Verbatim (rates):** non-residents pay on Honduran-source gross income: 1) rent of movable/immovable property **25%**; 2) mining/quarry/natural-resource royalties **25%**; 3) "Sueldos, salarios, comisiones o cualquier otra compensación por servicios prestados ya sea adentro del territorio nacional o fuera de él, excluidas las remesas" **25%**; 4) branch/subsidiary/agency profits of foreign enterprises **10%**; 5) "Rentas, utilidades, dividendos o cualquiera otra forma de participación de utilidades o reservas" **10%**; 6) patents/designs/secret formulas/trademarks/copyright royalties **25%**; 7) interest on commercial operations, bonds, securities **10%**; 8) air/sea/land transport operating income **10%**; 9) communications, "uso de software, soluciones informáticas, telemáticas" **10%**; 10) insurance/bond premiums **10%**; 11) public-show income **25%**; 12) films/videotape/cable rights **25%**; 13) "Cualquier otro ingreso de operación" **10%**. Payers are withholding agents per "los artículos 50 y 51 de la Ley" (p.6). Numeral-7 interest: recognized at 10% "cuando se paguen a entidades no relacionadas directa o indirectamente, caso contrario se gravarán como dividendos" (p.6). Capitalización de Reservas exempt (p.6).
- **Gloss:** definitive-looking gross-basis retention schedule for non-residents (character — definitive vs anticipO — cross-check with Ayuda 138 at evidence: OQ-3).
- **Candidate CRs:** non-resident income-category withholding engine (13 categories); related-party interest reclassification to dividend treatment.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting
- **Doubts/xref:** interplay with D. 17-2010 (ganancias de capital 10% / retención 1% anticipo) — does 17-2010 displace any numeral for residents' capital income? → OQ-2.

- **Loc:** Art. 7 (pp.6-7).

## EVID-005 Ley ISR — definiciones y pagos a cuenta (Art. 8)

- **Loc:** Art. 8 (pp.7-8).
- **Verbatim:** "Pagos a Cuenta": "Cada una de las cuatro cuotas trimestrales de pago del impuesto sobre la renta"; sujetos = all Art. 2 persons EXCEPT "aquellas personas cuyos ingresos provengan del trabajo personal y que sus respectivos impuestos están sujetos a retención en la fuente mensual" (Art. 8.i-j). Resident = lives in country, not "un mero transeúnte"; ship crews under Honduran flag = residents (Art. 8.d).
- **Gloss:** salary-only taxpayers are excluded from advance-installment regime (monthly withholding replaces it).
- **Candidate CRs:** pagos-a-cuenta eligibility gate (labor-income-only taxpayers excluded).
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-006 Ley ISR — renta bruta, ganancias de capital 10% y rentas no gravables (Art. 10)

- **Loc:** Art. 10 (pp.9-10).
- **Verbatim:** renta bruta = total yearly income from whatever source. "Las ganancias de capital obtenidas por las personas naturales o jurídicas, domiciliadas o no en Honduras, pagarán un impuesto único del 10%... por lo que no estarán sujetas a la tarifa progresiva" (p.9). State payments for purchase of goods, indemnities, rights/titles purchase: State withholds 10% as capital-gains tax (p.9). Not in renta bruta: a) insurance payouts from Honduran institutions; c) State/municipal subsidies; d) inheritance/legacy/donation values; e) national lottery prizes; f) occupational-risk indemnities and IHSS benefits; g) pension-fund investment income (Honduran institutions); h) "El valor de las prestaciones laborales, bonificación por vacaciones ordinarias de conformidad con el Código del Trabajo hasta con un pago adicional de treinta (30) días, jubilaciones, pensiones y montepíos" + contributions thereto deductible; **"El décimo tercer mes en concepto de aguinaldo, así como el décimo cuarto mes de salario, hasta por el monto de diez (10) salarios mínimos promedio, en cada caso, a partir de cuyo monto serán gravables"** (p.10).
- **Gloss:** capital gains = flat 10% final (unique impuesto) for everyone (detailed mechanics in D. 17-2010). Payroll: 13th AND 14th month each exempt up to 10 × average SMM, excess taxable; vacation bonus extra 30 days exempt; IHSS benefits/occupational indemnities exempt.
- **Candidate CRs:** capital-gains 10% separate track; payroll exemption engine (aguinaldo/14th-month 10-SMM caps with excess taxable — cap/threshold semantics: exempt "hasta por el monto de" then taxable from there → OQ-4 exact excess mechanics); non-grossable income flags.
- **Topics:** taxation, payroll
- **Doubts/xref:** labor-law source of décimo cuarto mes — Código del Trabajo wave (86_); D. 47-2024 interplay none (fondo reserva aportes not renta per its Art. 14).

## EVID-007 Ley ISR — deducciones empresariales (Art. 11)

- **Loc:** Art. 11 (pp.10-13).
- **Verbatim:** renta neta gravable of an empresa mercantil = bruta minus "gastos ordinarios y necesarios en la generación de la renta gravable... debidamente comprobados y pagados o incurridos": a) reasonable salaries + normal business expenses; b) insurance premiums (insurer must keep "por lo menos el 75% de sus reservas matemáticas... invertidas en el país"); c) interest on debt contracted to produce the income; d) depreciation per AT-approved method; e) taxes/contributions "con excepción del impuesto sobre la renta"; f) documented losses of income-producing property; g) bad-debt provision "El uno por ciento (1%) del valor de las ventas de bienes o servicios al crédito" per period, cap "nunca será superior al diez por ciento (10%) del importe de las cuentas por cobrar a clientes al cierre" (excluding related-party AR); incobrabilidad presumed at ">24 meses desde la fecha de su vencimiento"; recoveries of prior-year write-offs = taxable income; not deductible: intra-family/related-party (spouses, 4th-degree blood/2nd affinity, partners/directors) receivables; h) 10%-per-year-for-10-years amortization of free worker housing (hygiene conditions); i) 20%-per-year-for-5-years amortization of social/hygienic/cultural works for workers; j) employee gratuities "siempre que no excedan del total del sueldo devengado por el beneficiario durante el período de seis meses"; k) depletion amortization; l) donations to State/Distrito Central/municipalities/education/charity/sports (recognized entities); m) social-security quotas to autonomous funds (no enterprise benefit, AT-approved, national institutions); n) representation expenses + bonuses that are part of salary of owners/partners/executives — deductible "únicamente para transacciones específicas... relacionadas con empresas no vinculadas"; taxable to the recipient natural person (except documented representation expenses); company-paid personal assets/education for executives = taxable income to them (exception: collective-contract medical insurance etc.).
- **Gloss:** classic ordinary+necessary deduction chassis; notable HN specifics: 1%/10% bad-debt provision, worker-housing 10×10% and social-works 20%×5 amortizations, 6-month-salary gratuity cap, representation-expense dual treatment.
- **Candidate CRs:** deduction engine with HN-specific limits (bad-debt 1% accrual / 10% AR cap / 24-month presumption; gratuity cap; housing/social amortizations); related-party expense exclusions.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-008 Ley ISR — gastos no deducibles y deducciones personales (Arts. 12-13, 16-17)

- **Loc:** Art. 12 (p.13), Art. 13 (pp.13-14), Art. 16 (p.14), Art. 17 (p.14).
- **Verbatim:** non-deductible (Art. 12): a) repairs of wear already depreciated; b) "Las inversiones"; c) personal/family expenses (except next article); d) gifts/participations to partners or their relatives up to 4th degree (partner salaries deductible only as real, necessary, proportionate retribution — AT judgment); e) interest on capital invested/lent by owners/relatives/partners; f) capital losses (except Art. 21 [derogated]) and luxury/recreation losses. Personal deductions (Art. 13, residents only per Art. 16, monthly-prorated per Art. 17): a) "La suma anual hasta de CUARENTA MIL LEMPIRAS (L.40,000.00)" education + medical (doctors, labs, dentists, hospitals, medicines, resident professionals), "sin necesidad de presentar comprobante alguno"; ≥65 years old: L.80,000; b) documented profession/office/workshop expenses; c) farmers/ranchers production+maintenance, credit interest, depreciation; d) donations (same beneficiaries as 11.l) capped "hasta por un monto que no exceda al diez por ciento (10%) de la Renta Neta Gravable".
- **Gloss:** L40k/L80k personal deduction is a flat no-receipt allowance (remarkable for config: fixed L amounts, no IPC clause here — but 2025+ value question OQ-5); investment capitalization (b) — assets depreciated/amortized, not expensed.
- **Candidate CRs:** personal-deduction fixed amounts (L40k / L80k@65+); donation 10%-of-net cap; partner-payment reasonableness gates.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-009 Ley ISR — arrastre de pérdidas (Art. 20)

- **Loc:** Art. 20 (p.15).
- **Verbatim:** agropecuaria/agro-industrial/manufacturing/mining/tourism activities may carry operating losses forward "en los tres años de ejercicio siguiente", each year's amortization "que no exceda de CINCUENTA POR CIENTO (50%) de la renta neta gravable del año impositivo"; multi-activity taxpayers: "el arrastre y compensación de la pérdida sólo se hará contra las utilidades de la misma actividad que la originó"; requires AT authorization + annual DJ attaching P&L "certificado por un Perito Mercantil y Contador Público, debidamente colegiado"; non-deductible Art. 12 expenses excluded from the loss.
- **Gloss:** sector-limited, 3-year, 50%-cap, same-activity NOL — unlike SV (no general NOL).
- **Candidate CRs:** NOL ledger with sector + same-activity + 50% annual cap + 3-year expiry; certified-P&L attachment gate.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-010 Ley ISR — tarifas: PJ 25%, PN progresiva IPC-indexada, transporte presuntivo (Art. 22, 23)

- **Loc:** Art. 22 (pp.16-17), Art. 23 (p.21).
- **Verbatim:** a) "Las personas jurídicas pagarán una tarifa de veinticinco por ciento (25%) sobre el total de la renta neta gravable". b) PN progressive scale FY2025 (post SAR-07-2025): Exentos L0.01–**L217,493.16**; 15% to **L331,638.50**; 20% to **L771,252.38**; 25% above. "Esta escala de tasas progresivas será ajustada automáticamente de forma anual a partir del año 2017... aplicando la variación interanual del Índice de Precios al Consumidor (IPC)... Asimismo, estos valores se ajustarán en los artículos de la presente Ley que hagan referencia a los mismos" (p.16). c) foreign transport (air/sea/land) companies operating in-country: presumed net base "equivalente al diez por ciento (10%) del total de los ingresos brutos anuales de fuente hondureña", then 25% rate. Art. 23: PN exemption of first L217,493.16 (2025 vintage, rides the Art. 22.b auto-adjustment).
- **Gloss:** the IPC auto-adjustment clause explains the annual SAR-acuerdo table series (07_/08_/09_/10_/12_ + 89_ chain in Cuadro 2) — the LAW pins the mechanism, the acuerdos pin each year's values; FY2026 = SAR-01-2026 (+4.98%): exentos ≤ L228,324.32 / 15% ≤ L348,154.10 / 20% ≤ L809,660.75 (OCR-recovered, `07_`).
- **Candidate CRs:** dated progressive-scale rows (per-FY valid_from/valid_to, from SAR acuerdos, never overwritten); PJ 25%; transport 10%-presumed-net regime.
- **Topics:** taxation, payroll

## EVID-011 Ley ISR — Art. 22-A minimum-tax-on-gross regime (large taxpayers)

- **Loc:** Art. 22-A (pp.17-21).
- **Verbatim:** added by D. 278-2013; reformed D. 31-2018 + D. 31-2019; Instructivo Acuerdo 461-2020. Current (num. 3, FY2020+): taxpayers with prior-year gross ≤ **L1,000,000,000** are NOT subject to 22-A (ordinary Art. 22 applies); gross > L1,000M pay **1.0% of gross income** when ordinary Art. 22 tax < 1.0% of declared gross; reduced **0.5%** for sectors: cement; state public services; human pharma products (producer/importer/marketer); bakery; steel construction derivatives (not scrap/mining); coffee production/marketing/export. Excluded from 22-A (num. 4): a) natural persons with salary income; b) written press; c) companies in first 2 years/pre-operational until first sale; d) force-majeure/natural-disaster/war losses (2 exercises, auditor-certified); e) petroleum producers/distributors; f) those under the applicable gross thresholds. Num. 6: companies with gross ≥ L100M declaring operating losses in 2 alternate/consecutive periods → D. 96-2012 regime. Num. 7 + procedure: SEFIN favorable resolution (petition by Apr 30, 3 prior pagos-a-cuenta paid, external tax-audit report by CNBS A/B firm distinct from financial auditor; SEFIN resolves ≤60 días hábiles; reposición/revisión resources; AT bound). Resulting tax = pagos-a-cuenta base (num. 5). 22-A credit balance applicable to any AT tax or cessionable per CT Art. 142.
- **Gloss:** gross-income minimum tax interacting with ordinary rate (max(normal, %×gross) semantics with sector reductions and threshold vintages 2018/2019/2020+); SEFIN escape-hatch procedure.
- **Candidate CRs:** 22-A comparator engine (dated thresholds L300M/L600M→L1,000M by FY; sector catalog; exclusions); 22-A credit ledger + cession pointer; SEFIN-resolution status flag.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-012 Ley ISR — dividendos 10%, anticipos y holding (Arts. 25-26)

- **Loc:** Art. 25 (pp.21-22), Art. 26 (p.22).
- **Verbatim:** dividends or any participation of profits/reserves to residents: "estarán gravados con un Impuesto del Diez Por Ciento (10%), el cual será retenido y enterado por la sociedad mercantil"; excluded from the progressive scale; "queda exento del pago de cualquier impuesto la capitalización de reservas o utilidades". Deemed dividends: declared-but-unpaid at 17-2010 vigencia; **anticipo**: "cuentas por cobrar a socios o empresas relacionadas que no surjan de una operación comercial y que tengan un plazo mayor a cien (100) días calendario" (p.21). No double retention on downstream distribution of already-taxed dividends — "las personas jurídicas llevarán cuenta separada de las cantidades que reciban por dicho concepto, con indicación del origen" (p.22). Holding companies (sole activity = share ownership, real management direction, participation in each held company): redistributed dividends not re-taxed; SEFIN registry/alignment within 30 days (p.22). Art. 26: PJ must withhold "en las fechas en que se efectúen los pagos o créditos"; deposit "dentde los diez (10) días calendario del mes siguiente" [sic — "dentro"] to TGR/SEFIN-authorized institutions.
- **Gloss:** 10% dividend withholding with anti-conduit (100-day related receivables = deemed dividend) and holding/participation-exemption ledger.
- **Candidate CRs:** dividend-withholding 10% engine; deemed-dividend detector (related-party >100-day receivables); exempt-capitalization event type; holding-company separate account + no-double-retention guard.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-013 Ley ISR — ejercicio, declaración anual, pagos a cuenta y enteros (Arts. 27-29, 34)

- **Loc:** Art. 27 (p.23), Art. 28 (pp.23-24), Art. 29 (p.24), Art. 34 (pp.26-27).
- **Verbatim:** fiscal year = Jan 1–Dec 31 (special period allowed with prior written notice, Art. 27). Annual DJ: "del uno de enero al treinta de abril o siguiente día hábil" (Art. 28); filing exemptions: PN with gross ≤ L217,493.16 and "los contribuyentes asalariados cuyos ingresos provengan de una única fuente de trabajo y que hayan satisfecho totalmente el pago... mediante el Sistema de Retención en la Fuente"; empresas attach certified Balance General + P&L (contador titulado); special-period taxpayers file within 3 months of close; non-residents get 3-month extension. Art. 29: DJ shows own computation credited against pagos a cuenta; **December Manifestación de Pagos a Cuenta** for next year (new taxpayers: within 30 days of starting); quarterly minimum = average of last 3 years' tax. Art. 34: payment in 4 quarterly installments — cuotas 1-3 due **30-jun / 30-sep / 31-dic**, cuota 4 = balance due **30-abr** (or next hábil); special-period quarterly = last day of each quarter of their period; annual pagos a cuenta < L200 → accumulate to single payment **30-nov**; AT may rectify cuotas on request (extraordinary income / foreseen losses); credit balances apply to next year's cuotas; withholding agents: monthly DJ + enter "a más tardar dentro de los diez (10) días calendario del mes siguiente" (also Art. 26 for dividends).
- **Gloss:** the calendar spine: annual DJ Apr-30 window (rides SAR-619-2024 EEFF-previo requirement for FY2024+), December manifestation, quarterly advance dates, monthly retention entero 10-days-calendar.
- **Candidate CRs:** fiscal-calendar engine (DJ window, manifestation, 4 cuotas, 10-day entero); filing-exemption gates; 3-year-average advance computation; <L200 consolidation rule.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-015 Ley ISR — retención 12.5% servicios y cobranza (Arts. 50-51)

- **Loc:** Art. 50 (pp.30-31), Art. 51 (p.31).
- **Verbatim:** AT may order third-party payers to withhold from a delinquent taxpayer ("igual al impuesto adeudado más el interés moratorio" per CT Art. 163). Withholding-at-source authorized as collection method generally. Payments to non-residents per Arts. 4/5/25: withholding mandatory. "Las personas jurídicas de derecho público y derecho privado, que efectúen pagos o constituyan créditos a favor de personas naturales o jurídicas residentes en Honduras, no exoneradas del Impuesto Sobre la Renta deberán retener y enterar al Fisco el doce punto cinco por ciento (12.5%) del monto de los pagos o créditos que efectúen por concepto de honorarios profesionales, dietas, comisiones, gratificaciones, bonificaciones y remuneraciones por servicios técnicos" — exception: payments "bajo contratos de trabajo celebrados dentro del ejercicio fiscal y cuyos honorarios como única fuente de ingresos no excedan los... - **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting, payroll
