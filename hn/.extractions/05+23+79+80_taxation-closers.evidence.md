## EVID-062 05_ — ganancias de capital 10%: mechanics (E-Art 14, pp.7-9)

- **Loc:** E-Art 14 (pp.7-9), reformed D. 278-2013 Art. 13 + D. 170-2016 Art. 195.
- **Verbatim:** capital gains by natural/juridical persons, resident or not: **tarifa única 10%**, NOT subject to the progressive ISR tariff. Payment "por cada transacción dentro de los diez (10) días hábiles siguientes en que se percibió el valor pactado"; **annual declaration by April 30** of the following fiscal year; capital losses offset only against capital gains **of the same fiscal year**. Scope: transfer/cesion/compra-venta/negotiation of goods or rights by persons whose habitual business is NOT trading those goods; **enajenación of the taxpayer's habitual dwelling excluded**. Permuta: both parties subject, each computes its gain at transfer value. Transaction value = agreed value provided not below market, **"deduciendo las mejoras comprobadas y gastos de legalización"**. **Non-resident transfers of real estate/rights/securities: the acquirent must retain 4% of the transmission value** "a cuenta de este impuesto", entero within 10 días calendario of the transaction. Reorganization exception: fusión/absorción/cesión/escisión "al momento de su cambio" (no capital-gains tax at reorganization). Excluded losses: gambling and self-consumption. Not capital gains/losses: 1) gananciales separation; 2) copropiedad division (historical value preserved); 3) capital reduction with devolution **"siempre que sean utilidades no distribuidas previamente"**.
- **Gloss:** current authority for the 10% itself = `01_` Art. 10 (EVID-006 there); 05_ Art. 14 = the mechanics-rich text (per-transaction 10-días-hábiles clock, Apr-30 annual, same-year loss rule, value formula, non-resident 4% retention). Authority interplay OQ below. Non-resident 4% real-estate retention = a distinct retention trigger for buyers (complement to 01_ EVID-003 non-resident table).
- **Candidate CRs:** capital-gains per-transaction payment ledger + Apr-30 annual declaration; non-resident real-estate 4% buyer-retention engine; reorganization no-tax flag; habitual-dwelling + non-events exclusion guards.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-064 05_ — Importación Temporal (RIT) reformed text: suspension + guarantees (E-Art 30, pp.13-15)
