## EVID-040 D.17-2010 — dividendos: retention mechanics (L-Art 3 + R-Arts 1-6, pp.2-3/2)

- **Loc:** L-Art 3 (pp.2-3), R-Arts 1-6 (pp.2, both docs).
- **Verbatim:** 10% on dividends/participations of natural-person residents + dividends to naturales from special-regime societies; **retención única y definitiva** by the sociedad; excluded from the Art. 22 progressive base; in-kind distributions: base = market value (R-Art 2). Retention born "en el momento en que el dividendo... sean acordadas por el órgano competente... sean pagados, acreditados o puestos a disposición" — earliest of agreement/payment/credit/availability (R-Art 5). **Deemed dividends:** (a) related-party receivables >100 días not from commercial operations — **only those created AFTER 17-2010 vigencia** (R-Art 4); lending companies' operations ARE commercial (R-Art 1.a); (b) capital reductions with distribution — first amounts deemed capitalized-reserve distributions "hasta agotar la cuantía" (R-Art 1.b). Entero: first 10 days of following month; **agent declares in the "Declaración Anual de Retenciones (DAR)"** (R-Art 5). Capitalization exempt when approved and inscribed in Libro de Actas (R-Art 6).
- **Candidate CRs:** dividend-retention engine (agreement-date trigger, in-kind market-value base); deemed-dividend detectors (100-day related receivables post-vigencia, capital-reduction waterfall); DAR annual report; holding separate-account cross-ref (01_ EVID-012).
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-042 D.17-2010 — impuesto cedular alquiler de viviendas 10% (L-Art 5 + R-Arts 9-13)

- **Loc:** L-Art 5 (pp.3-4), R-Arts 9-13 (pp.3-4).
- **Verbatim:** "impuesto anual específico de renta único y definitivo al diez por ciento (10%) como cédula" on the price of renting housing/apartment buildings with rent **> L.15,000 monthly aggregated across all units of the same property** ("como cuota única o como monto total pagado entre todas las unidades habitacionales o cuartos de un mismo inmueble"); base = gross rental, "sin aplicar deducciones" (R-Art 11). Exceptions: hotels/motels/hospedajes (declare under ISR ordinary rules); pensiones/mesones/cuarterías per Ley de Inquilinato D. 50-1979. Mixed dwelling+commercial leases: contract-split if itemized and realistic; else global rent × (dwelling m² / total m²); exempt-threshold test uses ONLY the dwelling portion (R-Art 11). Subjects: natural-person landlords; co-owners = single subject with representative (DEI designates if absent); mixed natural+juridical ownership splits income proportionally, natural part pays the 10% cédula and the L15,000 threshold does NOT apply per-part (R-Art 10). **Agents of retention: trust administrators over rental property, the owner or designated administrator, or the mandatario** (R-Art 13); non-retained cases declare under ISR Art. 28 procedure (R-Art 12). Feeds código 106 (Ayuda `31_`).
- **Candidate CRs:** rental-cedulla engine (property-level aggregation, m² proration, mixed-ownership split, gross base); agent appointment flags.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-045 D.17-2010 — retención 1% anticipo sobre compras (R-Arts 16-19 + D.28-2019)

- **Loc:** R-Arts 16-19 (pp.4-5), D.28-2019 Art. 1 (p.3 of `22_`); L-Art 19 (p.6) for the minimum-tax side.
- **Verbatim:** **1% retention as ISR ANTICIPO on purchases of goods and services**: base = purchase price minus returns/discounts/rebates; **monthly accumulation per supplier allowed** ("podrá acumular los montos de las compras de mes, realizadas a un mismo proveedor") (R-Art 16); consignment goods: retention only when the purchase materializes (partial allowed). **Exempt suppliers:** pagos-a-cuenta subjects; also no retention on continuous utility supply (acueducto/alcantarillado), electric energy, fuels, financial services and insurers, artisanal products, fresh seasonal fruits/vegetables bought directly from producer, + DEI-designated others; unverifiable supplier status → retain (R-Art 17). **Obligated retainers: personas jurídicas y comerciantes individuales with annual sales > L15,000,000** (R-Art 18); entero within 10 days of following month; **constancia de retención issued on request** — creditable in the annual ISR declaration. Loss-making retained companies may request devolución/credit after fiscalización (R-Art 19). **D. 28-2019 interprets L-Art 19:** garbage/waste-collection services (municipal garbage, public ways, door-to-door) are NOT subject to the 1% retention — no venta elements (possessio/use/enjoyment) per the ISV law; therefore persons dedicated to waste collection are excluded.
- **Gloss:** the compras-anticipo retention (códigos 135/1% — Ayudas `37_`/`39_`, DJIMR detail); threshold L15M for the retainer; supplier-side exemptions; waste-collector carve-out.
- **Candidate CRs:** purchase-retention engine (1%, monthly per-supplier aggregation, L15M retainer gate, exemption catalog + waste-collector class); constancia issuance; loss-year refund workflow.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting, payroll(no)
