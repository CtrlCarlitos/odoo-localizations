## EVID-016 Ley ISV — creación, venta y base imponible (Arts. 1-4)

- **Loc:** Art. 1 (p.4), Art. 2 (p.4), Art. 3 (pp.4-5), Art. 4 (p.5).
- **Verbatim:** tax on sales "en forma no acumulativa en la etapa de importación y en cada etapa de venta" (Art. 1). Venta = any onerous transfer of dominion regardless of contract name/payment form; INCLUDES taxable services AND "el consumo o uso por el importador de las mercaderías que introduzca al país, o por el industrial o productor de las mercaderías o productos que, respectivamente, elabore o produzca" unless expressly exempt (Art. 2). Base (Art. 3): a) goods/services = value excluding "gastos directos de financiación ordinaria o extraordinaria"; b) imports = **CIF + arancelarios + selectivos + específicos + demás cargos**; c) self-use/self-supply/gifts = commercial value. NOT in base: "descuentos efectivos que consten en la factura o documento equivalente" (customary) + returnable packaging. Art. 4: absent/invoice-below-market → AT uses "el precio de mercado en plaza" salvo prueba en contrario.
- **Gloss:** multi-stage non-cumulative (VAT-family) tax; self-supply taxable; finance charges excluded from base; floor at market price.
- **Candidate CRs:** base-imponible engine (finance-charge exclusion, import composition, self-supply valuation, discount guards); market-price floor.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-017 Ley ISV — related-party pricing and hecho generador (Arts. 5, 5-A)

- **Loc:** Art. 5 (p.5), Art. 5-A (pp.5-6).
- **Verbatim:** sales to administrators/council members/socios/comisarios/relatives/cónyuge: "se gravarán a los precios normales de plaza" (Art. 5). Taxable event (Art. 5-A): a) goods = "la fecha de emisión de la factura o documento equivalente y, a falta de éste, en el momento de la entrega" (even with reserva de dominio/retroventa); b) services = invoice date OR service date OR payment/abono — "dependiendo de cual se realice primero"; c) self-use/fixed-asset incorporation = retirement date; d) imports = nacionalización/póliza payment; e) used goods taxed only on import (then a cost); internal resale of used goods NOT taxed.
- **Gloss:** invoice-date-first trigger (delivery fallback) differs from accrual-style regimes; used-goods no-retaño rule.
- **Candidate CRs:** taxable-event stamper (invoice-else-delivery for goods; earliest-of for services); related-party plaza-price override; used-goods flow (cost on import, exempt resale).
- **Topics:** taxation, e-invoicing

## EVID-018 Ley ISV — tasa 15%, 18% sinselectivo sectors, exportaciones tasa cero (Art. 6)

- **Loc:** Art. 6 (pp.6-8).
- **Verbatim:** "La tasa general del impuesto es del quince por ciento (15%)". Telecom scales all print 15% (post-reform homogenization; historial scales retained in text). **18%**: "Cuando este impuesto se aplique en la importación o venta de cerveza, aguardiente, licor compuesto y otras bebidas alcohólicas, cigarrillos" — beer/sodas: applied "sobre el precio de venta en la etapa de distribuidor, incluyendo el valor del impuesto de producción y consumo"; collection "a nivel de productor y en la importación al momento de liquidación y pago"; cigarettes: wholesale-stage price basis. Domestic air tickets: collected at emission/boarding point; airlines = responsible withholding agents, deposit "dentro de los diez (10) días calendario siguientes al mes"; **international+national tickets in business/first/class-beyond-economy = 18%**. Exports: "se calculará a tasa cero, quedando exentas las exportaciones y con derecho a crédito o devolución por el impuesto sobre ventas pagado en los insumos y servicios incorporados o utilizados en la producción de los bienes exportados, cuando el productor sea el mismo exportador".
- **Gloss:** 15% general / 18% selectivo-attached (alcohol/cigarettes + premium air seats); zero-rate export with input-credit (producer-exporter condition); producer/importer-level collection for the 18% family.
- **Candidate CRs:** rate matrix (15/18/0) with stage rules; export zero-rate credit ledger (producer-exporter gate).
- **Topics:** taxation, special-regimes

## EVID-020 Ley ISV — responsables, OTCD card-retention 10% and AT-designated retention (Art. 8)

- **Loc:** Art. 8 (pp.9-12).
- **Verbatim:** responsables: sellers, service providers, importers/customs agents. Simplified-regime sellers ≤ L250,000 annual: not collection agents, only "Declaración Anual de Ventas a más tardar el 31 de enero". AT may appoint productores/wholesalers as percepción agents (consumer-final-price base → goods then not re-taxed downstream). **Card issuers/operators/concessionaires (OTCD) = ISV retention agents**: automatic "retención del diez por ciento (10%) sobre el monto total del Impuesto Sobre Ventas que sea discriminado" in affiliates' taxable transactions; remainder reimbursed; retention comprobante issued so affiliates keep credit rights. Complementary obligations: OTCD solidarity only for reported ops; systems must force ISV discrimination "incluso cuando el mismo sea igual a cero (0)"; affiliates must register ISV even at zero (else OTCD auto-does); **no discrimination → OTCD applies 15% automatically**; all-exempt affiliates need AT exclusion resolution communicated to OTCDs; monthly banking-system entero "dentro de los primeros diez (10) días calendario del mes siguiente"; admin/civil/penal liability. AT also empowered to appoint habitual buyers of goods/services as retention agents (total or partial).
- **Gloss:** the ATC (agente tarjetas) regime — 10% ISV retention on discriminated tax, 15% presumption when not discriminated; feeds códigos 215/523 declarations (`41_`/`42_`); base procedure DEI-9382-J-2003 as modified by SAR-240-2024 (`19_`).
- **Candidate CRs:** OTCD 10%-of-tax retention engine + 15% no-discrimination fallback; zero-discrimination invoice mandate; exclusion-resolution workflow; AT-designated habitual-buyer retentions (config-driven).
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-022 Ley ISV — declaración mensual, Régimen Simplificado (Arts. 11, 11-A)

- **Loc:** Art. 11 (pp.12-13), Art. 11-A (pp.13-14).
- **Verbatim:** monthly DJ of sales + entero "dentro de los primeros diez (10) días calendario del mes siguiente" (p.13); declaration due even at zero/crédito-fiscal-favor balance or temporary closure. Simplified regime: single-establishment natural/juridical persons with taxable sales ≤ **L250,000/year** — no DJ; AT annual-ventas declaration only (31-ene); non-concurrence of requirements → ordinary rules; opt-in requires proving conditions in the 2 prior fiscal years; AT may reclasify de oficio; started-mid-year annualization = first-2-months sales ÷60 × 360; L250,000 base excludes exempt sales and factory-level-taxed goods.
- **Gloss:** the 10-calendar-day monthly cycle (same as retentions); simplified-regime gates (one establishment, ≤L250k taxable, 2-year history); código 202 declaration.
- **Candidate CRs:** monthly ISV DJ engine + 10-day entero; simplified-regime eligibility/annualization/reclassification; annual ventas declaration (31-ene).
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting

## EVID-023 Ley ISV — liquidación débito/crédito (Art. 12)

- **Loc:** Art. 12 (pp.14-17).
- **Verbatim:** liquidation = débito − crédito. Débito = rate × sales value, minus taxes returned for "ventas anuladas o rescindidas" and for rebates/discounts in the period. Crédito = ISV paid on imports + domestic purchases of goods/services, minus taxes refunded for cancelled purchases/price reductions. Credit rights: inputs "vinculados directamente con la producción"; **fixed-asset goods used to produce taxable consumption goods** (also intangibles, p.16); repair/maintenance services on fixed assets (not increasing value); services "indispensables para la producción, elaboración o venta" related to the activity; credit requires ISV actually PAID at purchase/import; **excluded: special export regimes' enterprises** (p.15); no credit for self-use/gifts or undocumented/non-compliant purchases; ISV credit never also an ISR cost/gasto "salvo cuando el impuesto sobre ventas pagado esté relacionado con operaciones exentas de este impuesto"; bad debts do NOT release the débito fiscal. Monthly declarants: credit controllable only in the causation period "o en uno de los tres períodos mensuales inmediatamente siguientes" (4-month credit window). Mixed taxpayers: linked credit 100%; unidentifiable → pro-rata "en el porcentaje correspondiente a las ventas gravadas del período", exempt-linked credit = costo/gasto. Favorable balance carries forward month to month. No cash refund except express cases (CT Arts. 129/131 or credit notes); credit non-transferable EXCEPT fusión/absorción continuing the giro; cessation forfeits remaining credit. Exporters' purchases: "Orden de Compra Exenta" mechanism for tax-free inputs (AT-granted).
- **Gloss:** full débito/crédito mechanics with HN specifics: 4-month credit window, pro-rata rule, ISR-cost bar with exempt-operations exception, non-transferability + merger exception, Orden de Compra Exenta for exporters.
- **Candidate CRs:** débito/crédito ledger with period-window credit rule; pro-rata mixed-activity splitter; merger credit succession; OCE (Orden de Compra Exenta) purchase flow; refund gate (CT 129/131).
- **Topics:** taxation
- **Doubts/xref:** pro-rata formula expressed as percentage-of-gravadas (sales-value based) — reglamento/practice may refine (generalidades 70_ check at fiscal-reporting wave).

## EVID-026 Ley ISV — territorialidad de servicios, registros y conservación (Arts. 17, 19)

- **Loc:** Art. 17 (p.20), Art. 19 (pp.20-21).
- **Verbatim:** service rendered in-country when it takes place "total o parcialmente, dentro de las fronteras de Honduras" regardless of parties' nationality/domicile (Art. 17). Responsables keep daily buy-sell accounting records; keep invoices/documents ≥5 years; simplified-regime persons: no accounting, but daily buy-sell annotations + same 5-year retention (Art. 19).

- **Candidate CRs:** service-source classifier (partial-presence rule); 5-year document-retention policy; simplified daily-book duty.
