## Tier 1 — payroll blockers

### 1. THE 2026 JD CEILING ACT  (TOP — unblocks the 2026 interim row)
The IHSS Junta Directiva resolution fixing 2026+ cotización ceilings per the
actuarial study (D. 48-2024 Art. 2 delegation). Until it lands, 2026 payroll
runs on a reversible interim row (2025 values carried forward).

- **Route A (primary):** `https://portalunico.iaip.gob.hn/356/83/` — browse
  the ENTIRE IHSS publication series; also probe sibling sections
  (`/356/81/`, `/356/82/`, `/356/84/`, `/356/85/`…). Look for 2026 releases
  mentioning: **techos de cotización, estudio actuarial, SOJD-IHSS,
  resolución junta directiva 2026**.
- **Route B (from Mon 24-ago-2026):**
  `https://enag.gob.hn/index.php/gaceta-digital/2026/septiembre` — open each
  day's issue → **Sección A → look for blocks headed
  "El infrascrito, Secretario de la Junta Directiva del IHSS… CERTIFICA"**
  (IHSS JD acts publish as certificaciones; seen in G 37,130/37,188/37,189).
- **What to save:** the full gazette PDF (even if 200 pp) or the release PDF.
- **How to recognize it:** a certified **Acuerdo/Resolución SOJD-IHSS-###-2026**
  whose subject includes *fijar los techos de cotización… sobre la base del
  estudio actuarial*.

### 2. RP CLASS CUADROS (riesgos profesionales rate table by class)
The flat 0.2% employer rate is pinned; the **class-differentiated rates**
(five risk classes, siniestralidad reviews) are the last RP config gap.

- **Where:** the same portalunico IHSS series (the ONLY live route left).
- **Search terms:** *cuadro de tarifas, clases de riesgo, tabla de primas,
  siniestralidad, reglamento de riesgos profesionales,ley de accidentes
  de trabajo*.
- **Also acceptable:** any Acuerdo/JD resolución printing class rates; the
  Reglamento General de Medidas Preventivas de Accidentes de Trabajo y
  Enfermedades Profesionales (the 145_ Arts. 88-97 chassis's sibling).

### 3. D. 92-2014 STATUTE (Ley de Reconocimiento de Cotizaciones Individuales
y Aportaciones entre Institutos Públicos de Previsión Social)
The multi-instituto election law (INPREMA/INJUPEMP/INPREUNAH/IPM vs IHSS).
We hold the INPREMA-side procedure (`183_`) + the IHSS comunicado quote —
**the statute text itself is wanted** (Art. 5 = the one-instituto election).

- **Where:** portalunico (IHSS + INPREMA sections);
  `https://biblioteca.tsc.gob.hn/` → search **92-2014**;
  Google: *Decreto 92-2014 Honduras cotizaciones previsión social*.
- **What to save:** the gazette original (G ~2014) or any official full-text
  print (congreso/SAR/instituto consolidation).

## Tier 2 — statutory pins & watches

### 4. CbCR RESTATEMENT COMUNICADO (SAR-653-2023 TRANSITORIO reviver)
SAR's Director-Ejecutivo comunicado reviving local CbCR filing (suspended
since 19-mar-2024 by the TRANSITORIO clause). Until it appears, the local
filing duty stays "suspended" in config.

- **Where:** `https://www.sar.gob.hn/` — **navigate visually** to
  Avisos / Noticias / Comunicados pages (their search API does NOT index
  banners/PDF notices). Look for: *comunicado país por país, 653-2023,
  Informe País por País*.

### 5. SEE / CAEE TECHNICAL DOCS (Sistema de Emisión Electrónica)
The e-invoicing electronic-emission specs (Arts. 50-58 chassis) — never
published in any corpus route (lead 1 since W1).

- **Where:** `https://www.sefin.gob.hn/` — **navigate menus, don't search**:
  Facturación / Emisión Electrónica / CAEE / comprobantes electrónicos.
  Also check SAR's facturación help pages for linked specs/manuals.
- **What counts:** any manual, instructivo, technical annex, or calendario
  de incorporación for the SEE/CAEE.

### 6. ACUERDO 461-2020 (SEFIN 22-A exception instructivo)
The operative SEFIN procedure for 22-A exception petitions (unpinned date).

- **Where:**
  `https://enag.gob.hn/index.php/gaceta-digital/2020/noviembre` and
  `.../2020/diciembre` — open each issue (~40 total), scan the **p.1
  SUMARIO** for *461-2020*, *Instructivo*, or *22-A*. Quick visual pass.
