## 1. Purpose

This file owns the fuel-quantity contribution layer of the
special-regimes wave (master cluster SR8): the FOVIAL *Contribución de
Conservación Vial* (road-conservation contribution) of **$0.20 per
galón** (gallon) on diesel, gasolinas y sus mezclas con otros
carburantes (gasoline and its blends with other fuels — aviation
gasoline and, since the D.L. 114-2006/2008 reform chain,
pesca-extractiva fuel excluded), whose corpus authority is NOW THE
INSTRUMENTS (W19 fold-in, 2026-08-22; SOQ-39 consumed): **86_** = the
consolidated Ley del Fondo de Conservación Vial (D.L. N° 208-2000
through reform (5) D.L. 93-2012) — Art. 26 owns the per-unit model
(hechos generadores: first-tier sale/transfer by
importadores/refinadores and direct import for own consumption at
entry; retention at the local sale; the monthly enter clock);
**87_/88_** = the D.O. prints fixing the value's provenance (the
ORIGINAL Art. 26 carried NO per-gallon value — a value-less Executive
traslado clause; the $0.20 per-unit model enters ONLY with D.L.
597-2001, vigencia 17-nov-2001); the DGII guide **31_** is RETAINED
as the treatment authority for the accounting/documentation surfaces
(separate fila/casilla, the B2B control-account chain — RETENCIÓN
FOVIAL at the first sale; CUENTAS POR COBRAR-FOVIAL re-billed through
every intermediate until the final consumer absorbs it, then
cost/operating/manufacturing-expense classification with the Ley ISR
Art. 29.6 own-consumption deduction); and the COTRANS layer: the
**$0.10/galón contribución especial para la estabilización de las
tarifas del Servicio de Transporte Público de Pasajeros Tipo
Colectivo y Masivo** (**89_** = D.L. 257-2021 — passenger-tariff
identity; the pre-W18 corpus gloss "empresas transportistas de carga"
is corrected W19), with its own retention/enter clock (3 días
hábiles post-month-close; 100% multa on the retained amount), the
FOVIAL×COTRANS stacking guard (89_ Art. 5) and the vigencia chain as
D15 dated rows (23-dic-2021 → 31-dic-2026 current end).

It does **not** cover: the Ley IVA computation mechanics themselves
(IVA-core wave territory — this file records the base-exclusion
invariant and the SOQ-40 design-pass pointer, never the engine); DTE
tributo-line generation and per-type CAT-015 restrictions
(`e-invoicing/01_document-types.md` SV-EINV-FR-017/SV-EINV-FR-046 by
id); the F-07 fuel annexes 13-14 and the casilla-525 credit interplay
(`fiscal-reporting/05_f07-annexes-special.md` SV-FREP-FR-124..129 and
`01_f07-declaration.md` SV-FREP-FR-020 by id — pointers only); the
ISR deduction surface (`taxation/02_isr-deductions.md` SV-TAX-FR-045
by id); the fuel price-cap/tasa-diferenciada regimes of Decreto 321
and the Ley Especial Transitoria (fiscal-reporting/05 by id); and the
regime profile/benefit-state surfaces of files 01-07 (FOVIAL/COTRANS
ride ordinary fuel transactions, not regime admissions).

  following the collection period, ANEXANDO UNA DECLARACIÓN JURADA
  DEL VOLUMEN FACTURADO Y/O TRANSFERIDO (a sworn declaration of the
  invoiced/transferred volume — fed by FR-167's quantity data).
  Modeled as a monthly obligation tracker per retention-agent
  company: period totals (galones × the dated $0.20 row), enter

  attachment. The 597-era late-enter INTEREST rule (BCR tasa activa
  promedio + 4 pts — LB-009) was the Art. 26 inciso final DEROGATED
  by D.L. 789-2008: NO instrument interest rule ships on this clock

## 6. Acceptance Criteria
