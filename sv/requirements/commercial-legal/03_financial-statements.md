## 1. Purpose

This file defines the El Salvador annual financial-statements regime of the
Código de Comercio (Commercial Code, CC): the fiscal-year-close statement
duty of Art. 441 — *balance general* (balance sheet) + *estado de
resultados* (income statement) + *estado de cambio en el patrimonio*
(statement of changes in equity), certified and DEPOSITED in the Registro
de Comercio with the Auditor's *dictamen* (opinion) and annexes; the S.A.
balance-cycle discipline of Arts. 282-286 — the three-month IMPRORROGABLE
preparation term from *clausura del ejercicio social* (close of the
corporate fiscal year), handoff to the *auditor externo* (external
auditor), the 30-day dictamen term, *junta* (general meeting) approval
with the *memoria anual* (annual report), Diario Oficial publication of
the approved gestión, and the DEPOSIT that alone produces effects against
third parties — "sin su depósito, no harán fe" (without deposit they make
no fe/are not probative) — with the Art. 286 final Ministerio de Hacienda
exception for ISR declarations; the Art. 474 threshold ladder —
US$12,000/US$34,000 *activo* (assets) steps for individual merchants,
sociedades and EIRL always depositing the full set; the balances register
of Arts. 459/442 (what the *registro de balances* conserves and the
*registro de Estados Financieros* content checklist); and the valuation
hierarchy of Arts. 443-444 — *Consejo de Vigilancia de la Profesión de
Contaduría Pública y Auditoría* (vigilance council of the CPA profession)
criteria with the *Normas Internacionales de Contabilidad* (International
Accounting Standards, NIC/NIIF) FALLBACK — plus the Art. 445 revaluation
reserve and its disambiguation from the ISR *reserva legal*.

It does **not** cover: merchant status, the Art. 411 obligation chassis,
matrícula and the registry architecture (`01_merchant-registration.md` —
consumed by id: FR-004 exemption profiles, FR-005 obligation III = this
file, FR-013 registry-entry model, FR-015 matrícula-precondition gate,
FR-016 presentation-effects anchor, FR-017 publication engine);
bookkeeping form, register legalization, the no-alteration regime and
retention (`02_accounting-books.md` — the statements register is retention
matrix row a there; the Art. 447 labor provision is its FR-026); society
types, statutory *reserva legal* rates (5%/7% per type) and auditor
appointment/incompatibilities (`04_society-types.md`, when it lands —
Arts. 289-291/39/123 are its zone); the NIIF-conformity accounting-model
requirements themselves (chart-of-accounts wave — this file supplies only
the C3 NIIF hook pointer note, §2); ISR declaration mechanics
(taxation wave); or DTE/e-invoicing surfaces (no norm in this article set
touches DTE generation — no SaaS layer rows; e-invoicing/D3 consumers
reach this file by id pointer, never the reverse derivation).

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 FY-close statement set and content (Arts. 441, 282)

- **SV-CML-FR-031:** The system shall provide, per merchant and fiscal
  year, the annual statement set of Art. 441: at the close of each
  *ejercicio fiscal* (fiscal year) the merchant's economic situation is
  established through the *balance general*, the *estado de resultados*
  and the *estado de cambio en el patrimonio*; for sociedad-type
  merchants the balance content precision of Art. 282 applies — exact
  state of each account, specification of *activo* and *pasivo*
  (assets/liabilities), the amount of *utilidades o pérdidas* (profits or
  losses) registered, with the profit-and-loss statement accompanying the
  balance. This is obligation Art. 411-III of the SV-CML-FR-005 map
  (owned here; the map itself is `01_merchant-registration.md` by id).
  (LB-001; LB-002; EVID-214)

### 3.2 The three-month IMPRORROGABLE cycle and the auditor dictamen (Arts. 283-284)

- **SV-CML-FR-032:** The system shall compute and enforce the Art. 283
  statement deadline: the balance general, resultados and
  equity-changes statements must be CONCLUDED within the term of THREE
  MONTHS counted from the *clausura del ejercicio social* (close of the
  corporate fiscal year) — a term the Code marks IMPRORROGABLE
  (non-extendible), which the system shall encode with exactly that
  quality: the deadline = fiscal-year close + 3 calendar months, with NO
  extension, pause or waiver parameter in the model, and overdue state
  surfacing as a breach, never as a slippage; responsibility lies with
  the *administrador único* or *junta directiva*, and delivery to the
  *auditor externo* with the justificatory annex documents is due at the
  latest immediately upon expiry of that term (delivery date tracked).
  (LB-003; EVID-214)
- **SV-CML-FR-033:** The system shall compute the Art. 284 auditor
  dictamen deadline: within THIRTY DAYS counted from the auditor's
  receipt of the balance and annexes, the dictamen is formulated with all
  observations and proposals the auditor judges convenient (dictamen
  date, receipt date and the 30-day deadline tracked; dictamen content
  itself is the auditor's professional act — the system records its
  existence and reference, never generates it).
  (LB-004; EVID-214)

### 3.3 Junta approval, the deposit and third-party effect (Arts. 285-286, 441)

- **SV-CML-FR-034:** The system shall track the Art. 285-286 approval
  gate for sociedad-type merchants: the balance with annexes, the
  vigilance organ's dictamen/observations/proposals and the *memoria
  anual* (detailed annual report on the administration's gestión) go to
  the shareholders, and the *junta general* resolves approval or
  rejection (rejection → new junta convened); upon approval of the
  gestión, publication in the *Diario Oficial* follows at latest FIFTEEN
  DAYS after the junta — a specific-law publication rule that overrides
  the default Art. 486 engine (SV-CML-FR-017 consumed by id: single
  D.O. publication, 15-day cap, no national-daily alternation required
  by this text). (LB-005; LB-006; EVID-214)
- **SV-CML-FR-035:** The system shall implement DEPOSIT as the
  third-party-effect condition: once the statements are approved by the
  junta and certified by the Auditor, accompanied by the certification
  evidencing the junta's approval (and, per the Art. 474 ladder,
  FR-037), they are deposited in the Registro de Comercio — and ONLY the
  deposit makes them effective against third parties; per Art. 441,
  *sin su depósito, no harán fe* (no deposit, no fe): an undeposited
  statement set in the system carries a non-opposable/no-fe marker, and
  every public or private institution requiring exhibition of the
  statements must be recorded as entitled to demand THE DEPOSITED ones
  (deposit linkage via the registry-entry model of SV-CML-FR-013, kind
  balance_deposit, presentation timestamp = effect anchor per
  SV-CML-FR-016; matrícula-precondition gate per SV-CML-FR-015 applies
  to the deposit).
  (LB-001; LB-006; LB-008; EVID-214)
- **SV-CML-FR-036:** The system shall encode the Art. 286 final
  exception exactly: the Ministerio de Hacienda is excepted from the
  deposited-statements exhibition duty SOLELY for the purposes of filing
  the *declaraciones de impuestos sobre la renta* (income-tax
  declarations) — for those purposes MH may require duly audited
  statements — without prejudice to MH later requiring exhibition of the
  deposited ones within its fiscalization powers; the exception is
  recorded as a narrow consumer-side carve-out (ISR filing), never as a
  relaxation of the deposit duty itself (ISR declaration mechanics owned
  by the taxation wave — pointer only).
  (LB-006; EVID-214)

### 3.4 Threshold ladder for the deposit package (Art. 474)

- **SV-CML-FR-037:** The system shall derive, per merchant and fiscal
  year, the Art. 474 deposit-package profile from the threshold ladder:
  (a) *comerciante individual* with *activo* ≥ US$12,000 — annual
  deposit of fiscal-year-end balances signed by the *propietario o
  representante legal* (owner or legal representative) AND the
  *contador* (accountant), no auditor certification; (b) *comerciante
  individual* with *activo* ≥ US$34,000 — additionally certified by an
  auditor meeting the Art. 290 requirements (auditor incompatibilities
  owned by `04_society-types.md` when it lands); (c) *sociedades
  mercantiles* and *EIRL* — ALWAYS the full set: cierre-de-ejercicio
  balance general signed by representante legal + contador + auditor
  externo, accompanying estados de resultados and de cambio en el
  patrimonio, with the Auditor's dictamen and annexes. The US$12,000 and
  US$34,000 thresholds are 2008-reform (29) USD code text —
  dated-but-current (§2), stored as dated configuration values, never
  hardcoded. Profile interacts with SV-CML-FR-004: the Art. 15
  (below-US$12,000 individuals) and Art. 20 (ag/professional
  collectives) exemption profiles remove obligation 411-III — no deposit
  duty while exempt; the simplified-book population's annual balance
  (SV-CML-FR-027) is a bookkeeping artifact, not a deposit package.
  (LB-007; EVID-214/216)

### 3.5 The balances register (Arts. 459, 442)

- **SV-CML-FR-038:** The system shall record the Art. 459 conservation
  scope of the *registro de balances* (one of the three registry books,
  SV-CML-FR-013 by id): fiscal-year-end balances + estados de resultados
  and de cambio en el patrimonio + the Auditor's dictamen and annexes,
  of those merchants OBLIGED to send them (the FR-037 ladder defines the
  obliged set) — every deposit entry linking the complete deposited
  package; registry-side conservation is the Registro de Comercio's own
  act (CNR), the system tracks the deposited package and its
  presentation anchor.
  (LB-008; EVID-214/217)
- **SV-CML-FR-039:** The system shall provide the Art. 442
  *registro de Estados Financieros* content checklist as the
  deposit-package completeness surface (working reading — OQ-001: the
  Código names the merchant-side statements register with this content
  list while Arts. 456-III/459 name the registry book "Registro de
  balances"; treated as the same register's content discipline): (I)
  ordinary balance generals; (II) extraordinary balance generals — early
  liquidation of the business, *suspensión de pagos o quiebra*
  (suspension of payments or bankruptcy — terminology only, SOQ-24
  vintage watch), disposition of law, or the merchant's will; (III)
  inventory summaries relative to each balance; (IV) summaries of the
  accounts grouped into each balance's line items; (V) the
  pérdidas-y-ganancias statement per balance; (VI) the equity-composition
  statement; (VII) any other statement necessary to show the merchant's
  economic and financial situation; (VIII) the form in which the
  distribution of gains or the application of net losses was verified
  (ordinal VIII read with the dividend ceiling of Art. 38 and the
  statutory-reserve duties — `04_society-types.md` when it lands).
  (LB-009; EVID-214)

### 3.6 Valuation criteria and revaluation (Arts. 443-445)

- **SV-CML-FR-040:** The system shall record, per merchant, the
  Arts. 443-444 valuation-criteria basis as a two-step hierarchy with a
  special-law third route: balance line items formed from the open
  accounts and asset-element estimation per the criteria/rules issued by
  the *Consejo de Vigilancia de la Profesión de Contaduría Pública y
  Auditoría*; IN DEFAULT THEREOF (*en su defecto*) the *Normas
  Internacionales de Contabilidad* (NIC) apply; and where the two
  concord neither — depending on the business's nature and the existence
  of *Bolsas de Valores o Bolsas de Productos* (securities/commodities
  exchanges) — the special laws' norms govern. Truthfulness and
  exactness-compatible-with-purpose are the stated content standard. The
  system stores the operative basis (consejo_criteria · nic_fallback ·
  special_law_route) as informational configuration — THE NIIF HOOK: the
  accounting-model/COA consequences are the chart-of-accounts wave's to
  derive when it lands (pointer note §2; no FR re-derivation here).
  (LB-010; LB-011; EVID-214)
- **SV-CML-FR-041:** The system shall implement the Art. 445
  revaluation-reserve discipline: revaluation of goods figuring in the
  balance is lawful when justified by real market conditions, EXCLUDING
  *materias primas, productos y mercancías* (raw materials, products and
  merchandise — blocked revaluation classes); a lawful revaluation
  REQUIRES the balance itself to show, with complete clarity, a reserve
  evidencing the revaluation (clear reserve presentation = balance
  invariant); and sociedades constituting this reserve may NOT dispose
  of it except at the moment of liquidation or upon selling the
  revalued goods (disposal guard on the reserve account; sale of a
  revalued good or the liquidation event — lifecycle pointer to
  `05_society-lifecycle.md` when it lands — releases it). Disambiguation
  (by id, §2): this CC revaluation reserve is a DIFFERENT institution
  from the society statutory *reserva legal* (`04_society-types.md`,
  Arts. 39/123 rates) and from the ISR-book *reserva legal* whose
  constitution register and 25% separate-liquidation reduction trigger
  are `sv/requirements/taxation/02_isr-deductions.md` SV-TAX-FR-063
  (computation: `03_isr-rates-gains.md` §3.6 SV-TAX-FR-101) — no
  taxation rule is derived or restated here.
  (LB-012; EVID-214)

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given a sociedad with *ejercicio social* closing 31-Dec,
  when the statement cycle is computed, then the statements deadline is
  31-Mar (close + 3 months), marked IMPRORROGABLE; an attempted
  extension of the deadline finds no parameter and the overdue state
  surfaces as a breach; the delivery-to-auditor record is due at latest
  at that same expiry (FR-032).
- **AC-002:** Given statements delivered to the auditor on 05-Mar, when
  the dictamen deadline is computed, then it falls on 04-Apr (receipt +
  30 days) and the dictamen reference is recorded against it (FR-033).
- **AC-003:** Given junta approval of the gestión on 20-Apr with the
  memoria anual and junta-approval certification attached, when
  publication is scheduled, then one Diario Oficial publication is due
  by 05-May (junta + 15 days) with no national-daily alternation
  required (specific-law override), and the deposit package becomes
  eligible (FR-034, FR-035).
- **AC-004:** Given a full set (balance + resultados + equity-change +
  dictamen + annexes + junta certification) recorded but NOT yet
  deposited, when third-party effect is evaluated, then the set carries
  the "no fe" (no-deposit-no-fe) marker; upon recording the deposit
  entry (registry presentation anchor stamped), the marker clears
  (FR-035).
- **AC-005:** Given a *comerciante individual* with *activo*
  US$13,000, when the deposit profile is derived, then the annual
  balance deposit is signed by propietario/representante legal AND
  contador with NO auditor certification; given *activo* US$40,000,
  then the auditor certification (Art. 290 requirements) is additionally
  required; given a sociedad or EIRL at any size, then the full set with
  representante legal + contador + auditor externo signatures, dictamen
  and annexes is always required (FR-037).
- **AC-006:** Given the same merchant below US$12,000 (or an Art. 20
  ag/professional collective), when the ladder interacts with the
  SV-CML-FR-004 exemption profiles, then no deposit duty exists and the
  annual balance of SV-CML-FR-027 (if any) is marked a bookkeeping
  artifact, not a deposit package (FR-037).
- **AC-007:** Given a deposit entry recorded for a merchant without a
  vigente matrícula, when the SV-CML-FR-015 gate runs, then the entry is
  blocked with the unmatriculated empresa identified (FR-035;
  SV-CML-FR-015).
- **AC-008:** Given MH requiring statements for an ISR declaration
  filing, when the exception is evaluated, then audited non-deposited
  statements satisfy THAT purpose only, the deposit duty stands, and a
  later fiscalization demand for the deposited ones is recorded as
  legitimate (FR-036).
- **AC-009:** Given a deposit package under construction, when the
  Art. 442 checklist is evaluated, then all eight content ordinals
  (ordinary/extraordinary balances, inventory summaries, account-group
  summaries, PyG, equity composition, other necessary statements,
  gains-distribution/losses-application form) are present or explicitly
  N/A-justified before the package is marked depositable (FR-039).
- **AC-010:** Given no Consejo de Vigilancia estimation criteria
  configured, when the valuation basis resolves, then the basis falls
  back to *Normas Internacionales de Contabilidad* (nic_fallback) — and
  with Consejo criteria configured, the basis is consejo_vigilancia_criteria
  (FR-040).
- **AC-011:** Given an attempted revaluation of merchandise inventory,
  when the Art. 445 guard runs, then it is blocked (excluded class);
  given a lawful revaluation of a building justified by real market
  conditions, then the revaluation record requires a clearly-figured
  reserve account in the balance and the reserve's disposal is blocked
  until the liquidation event or the sale of that revalued good — and
  the record carries the disambiguation note separating it from the ISR
  *reserva legal* (SV-TAX-FR-063/101, by id) and the society statutory
  reserve (FR-041).
