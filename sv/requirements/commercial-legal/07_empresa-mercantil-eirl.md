## 1. Purpose

This file defines the El Salvador *empresa mercantil* (commercial
enterprise) regime of Book Three of the Código de Comercio (Commercial
Code, CC): the Art. 553 definition — a coordinated set of work, material
elements and incorporeal values offering goods or services to the public
systematically with profit purpose — with the Art. 554 character-survival
rule and the Art. 555 classification of the whole enterprise as a *bien
mueble* (movable asset), whose inmueble elements ride common law; the
Art. 556 unity-of-destination protection of the *elementos esenciales*
(essential elements) against disgregating creditor embargoes; the
Art. 557 statutory element package that every unexpressed contract over
an enterprise comprises by default (*establecimiento*, *clientela y fama
mercantil*, *nombre comercial y distintivos*, *arrendamientos*,
*mobiliario y maquinaria*, labor contracts, *mercancías y créditos*),
with IP-type elements only by express pact; the transmission regime of
Arts. 558-563 — debt succession to the acquirer, contract subrogation
with the third party's 3-month termination right, credit-cession effects
from Registry inscription, the >6-consecutive-months inactivity rule
that strips empresa character, and the transferor's two-year
non-compete; the *establecimiento* (commercial establishment) regime of
Arts. 565-569 — 15-day advance publication of local changes, the
creditor-acceleration rules on depreciation/plaza transfer/closure, and
lease survival on enterprise transfer; the IP-derogation scope note
(Arts. 570-599 — nombre comercial, distintivos, patentes DEROGATED to
special intellectual-property laws, no FRs); and the *Empresa Individual
de Responsabilidad Limitada* (EIRL, individual limited-liability
enterprise) vehicle of Arts. 600-622 — the Art. 600 default of unlimited
individual liability, the "E. de R. L." suffix rule, the
auditor-certified pre-constitution inventory with its asset
valuation/admissibility/cash-deposit rules, free capital, the
formulario-based seven-field registration and its inscription effects,
capital movements with the one-quarter-of-paid-capital reduction floor,
the certified-inventory publication with its 30-day opposition gate, the
obligations-coverage order and the *cuota suplementaria de garantía*
(supplementary guarantee quota), the Art. 615 lifting cases, the
Art. 616 incorporation-by-reference wiring (reserves, profits, financial
statements, vigilance — consumed from files 04/03 by id), voluntary
liquidation with the Código Tributario solvencia gate, forced-liquidation
triggers and judicial mechanics, traspaso rules, and the Art. 622

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 La empresa: definition, mueble character, statutory element package (Arts. 553-557)

- **SV-CML-FR-120:** The system shall model the *empresa mercantil*
  (commercial enterprise) as the Art. 553 asset-object — a coordinated
  set of work, material elements and incorporeal values offering goods
  or services to the public, with profit purpose and systematically —
  DISTINCT from the merchant record that exploits it (the comerciante
  identity of `01_merchant-registration.md` by id): the enterprise
  record's character SURVIVES variation of its elements and the lack of
  an *establecimiento* (commercial establishment) or permanent seat
  (Art. 554 — no establishment-required invariant), and the whole is
  classified as a *bien mueble* (movable asset) transferable and
  chargeable as one object (Art. 555), while the transmission and
  *gravamen* (encumbrance) of its REAL-ESTATE elements rides the rules
  of common law (real-estate law pointer — no computation; the Art. 614
  EIRL formalities of FR-135 govern the registry side).
  (LB-001; EVID-224)
- **SV-CML-FR-121:** The system shall implement the Art. 557 statutory
  element PACKAGE as the default content of every contract over an
  empresa mercantil that does not express the elements taken into
  account: (I) the establecimiento, if any; (II) the *clientela*
  (customer base) and *fama mercantil* (commercial goodwill); (III) the
  *nombre comercial* (trade name) and *distintivos comerciales*
  (commercial distinctive signs — textual reference survives the
  Arts. 570-585 derogation, §3.4); (IV) the lease contracts; (V) the
  furniture and machinery; (VI) the labor contracts per the laws
  applicable to the matter; (VII) the merchandise, credits and other
  similar goods and values — with invention patents, manufacturing and
  business secrets, exclusives and concessions comprised ONLY BY
  EXPRESS PACT (exclusion-default flags on the transfer record); and
  the Art. 556 UNITY protection: the *elementos esenciales* (the
  Art. 557 enumerated elements) may not be dismembered by individual
  creditor persecutions — an embargo must embrace the enterprise AS A
  WHOLE with an *interventor* (supervisor) as depositary charged
  against the cash box; an ISOLATED embargo of money, merchandise or
  credits is permitted only insofar as it does not impede the
  enterprise activity's continuation (informational invariant on
  asset-attachment surfaces); mortgage and pledge creditors are
  excepted.
  (LB-002; LB-003; EVID-224)

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given an enterprise-transfer contract recorded WITHOUT
  expressed elements, when the FR-121 package applies, then the
  transfer record carries all seven statutory elements (establecimiento
  with "if any" flag, clientela y fama, nombre y distintivos,
  arrendamientos, mobiliario y maquinaria, contratos de trabajo,
  mercancías y créditos) and NONE of the pacto-expreso class; given a
  recorded express pact adding "secretos del negocio", then that
  element — and only it — joins the package (FR-121).
- **AC-002:** Given an empresa whose last exploitation activity was
  01-Jan with no nature-justified suspension recorded, when the
  FR-124 clock evaluates on 05-Jul (6 months + 4 days, past day 183),
  then the record shows character_lost; given a check on 05-Jun, then
  the character remains active with the pre-expiry warning surfaced
  (FR-124).
- **AC-003:** Given a transmission from an individual merchant
  formalized by escritura pública inscribed in the Registro de
  Comercio, when the FR-122 record evaluates, then the acquirer is
  charged with the enterprise debts of the previous titular, each
  inherited payable carries the original-debtor recourse note, and a
  recorded pacto en contrario flags the exclusion (FR-122).
- **AC-004:** Given a transmission published 01-Mar whose third-party
  contractor declares justa causa termination on 30-May, when the
  FR-123 window computes, then the termination falls INSIDE the 3-month
  window (closes 01-Jun) and the enajenante-responsibility note
  attaches; given a debtor's good-faith payment to the enajenante
  after the transmission's inscription, then the release event is
  recorded (FR-123).
- **AC-005:** Given an establecimiento local change effective 01-Jun
  recorded without its 15-day advance publication, when FR-126
  evaluates, then the damages-exposure flag surfaces for
  establecimiento-traffic creditors; given the same change provably
  depreciating the establecimiento, then the 90-day creditor
  acceleration window (change date to +90 days) is recorded
  (FR-126).
- **AC-006:** Given an EIRL record named "María Pérez E. de R. L." and
  another named "María Pérez" (suffix omitted), when the FR-128
  validator runs, then the first passes and the second carries the
  suffix-defect warning with the unlimited-liability exposure of
  Art. 601; the enterprise record shows limited_eirl only when
  organized under the chapter (FR-128).
- **AC-007:** Given an EIRL constitution attempt whose inventory lacks
  the external-auditor certification, when the FR-129 gate runs, then
  the constitution is BLOCKED with the Art. 602 reason; given the
  conversion of a functioning unlimited empresa, then the inventory
  record requires the obligations side; given an inventory line for a
  título valor under a cancellation proceeding, then the FR-130
  admissibility blocker rejects it (FR-129, FR-130).
- **AC-008:** Given a formulario with fields I-V complete but field VI
  (capital + certified-inventory reference) blank, when the FR-131
  checklist runs, then the record is incomplete; given the formulario
  inscribed on 10-Sep, then effects run from 10-Sep and the
  pre-inscription unlimited-liability marker is lifted, with the
  matrícula obligation opening (SV-CML-FR-008 by id) (FR-131).
- **AC-009:** Given an EIRL with paid capital US$40,000 and a proposed
  reduction to US$9,000, when the FR-132 floor validator runs, then
  the reduction is BLOCKED (US$9,000 < 1/4 × US$40,000 = US$10,000)
  with the Art. 609 reason; given a reduction to US$10,500 meeting the
  Arts. 182-183 requisites after the Art. 610 window, then it
  formalizes via the reduction formulario and takes effect at
  inscription (FR-132, FR-133).
- **AC-010:** Given a certified constitution inventory published once
  in the Diario Oficial on 01-Apr with the national-daily publication
  the same day, when FR-133 computes, then the formulario is
  grantable from 02-May (30 days counted from the D.O. publication)
  and NOT before; given a creditor's opposition paid off on 20-Apr,
  then that opposition closes as of right by the payment event
  (FR-133).
- **AC-011:** Given an EIRL obligation unpaid at the patrimony level
  with a constituted guarantee quota, when the FR-134 coverage
  evaluates, then the coverage order shows enterprise patrimony first
  (capital + reservas + utilidades no retiradas) and the quota only
  after excusión; given the titular's personal creditor attempting an
  embargo on enterprise-ascribed goods, then the embargo-scope flag
  blocks it while the quota/utilidades routes remain open (FR-134).
- **AC-012:** Given a judicial declaration recorded that the EIRL was
  used with fraudulent ends, when FR-136 applies, then the
  liability_state transforms to unlimited with the Art. 615-I
  exposure; absent such a recorded declaration, then no lifting state
  is ever system-computed (FR-136).
- **AC-013:** Given an EIRL with net profits US$100,000 and reserve
  below 1/5 of capital, when the FR-137 wiring runs, then the 7%
  separation computes via 04's `eirl` profile row (FR-055 by id —
  never restated here), the 3-month statement deadline and full
  deposit package apply via 03's cycle (FR-032/FR-037(c) by id), and
  the auditor duty calendar attaches via FR-070 by id (FR-137).
- **AC-014:** Given an EIRL voluntary-liquidation record, when the
  FR-138 gate evaluates, then the CT solvencia/tributary-authorization
  checkpoint shows as an EXTERNAL gate wired to SV-CML-FR-090 (no CT
  mechanics computed, OQ-001 pointer), and the personas-rules
  reference points at 05's §3.4 machinery by id (FR-138).
- **AC-015:** Given an EIRL whose latest statements show a patrimony
  reduced to US$18,000 against capital social US$100,000 (below 1/4),
  when FR-139 computes, then the Art. 619-III forced trigger opens
  with a one-month cure window from the recorded knowledge date;
  given complementary aportaciones reintegrating the loss recorded
  inside that month, then the trigger is inapplicable and closes
  (FR-139).
- **AC-016:** Given a mortis-causa traspaso attempted before the
  declaratoria de herederos is inscribed, when the FR-140 ordering
  gate runs, then the traspaso inscription is blocked with the
  Art. 621-II sequence reason; given the enterprise functions under
  nombre and the heir keeps the former name, then the composed name
  carries "sucesores" (FR-140).
