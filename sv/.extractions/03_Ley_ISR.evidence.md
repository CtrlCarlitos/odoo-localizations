## EVID-088 Ley ISR — hecho generador y concepto de renta obtenida

- **Loc:** Art. 1 (p.1), Art. 2 (pp.2-3), D.L. 629 interpretación auténtica (pp.2-3).
- **Verbatim:** "La obtención de rentas por los sujetos pasivos en el ejercicio o periodo de imposición de que se trate, genera la obligación de pago del impuesto establecido en esta Ley." (Art. 1, p.1). Renta obtenida = productos/utilidades "percibidos o devengados... ya sea en efectivo o en especie y provenientes de cualquier clase de fuente" (Art. 2, p.2), classified: a) del trabajo (salarios, sueldos, honorarios, comisiones, "toda clase de remuneraciones o compensaciones por servicios personales"); b) actividad empresarial (comercial, agrícola, industrial, servicio); c) capital (alquileres, intereses, dividendos); d) catch-all: "TODA CLASE DE PRODUCTOS, GANANCIAS, BENEFICIOS O UTILIDADES CUALQUIERA QUE SEA SU ORIGEN, DEUDAS CONDONADAS, PASIVOS NO DOCUMENTADOS O PROVISIONES DE PASIVOS EN EXCESO, ASÍ COMO INCREMENTOS DE PATRIMONIO NO JUSTIFICADO Y GASTOS EFECTUADOS POR EL SUJETO PASIVO SIN JUSTIFICAR EL ORIGEN DE LOS RECURSOS A QUE SE REFIERE EL ARTÍCULO 195 DEL CÓDIGO TRIBUTARIO" (Art. 2.d, p.3). Pasivos no documentados / provisiones en exceso imputan al período respectivo. D.L. 629: gastos de representación of public officials are NOT remuneration (excluded from literal a).
- **Gloss:** taxable event = obtaining income in the fiscal period; income categories feed different computation tracks (ordinary vs capital vs 10% separate-liquidation rents). Cross-ref: CT Art. 195 (unjustified patrimony).
- **Candidate CRs:** income-category classification engine (trabajo/empresarial/capital/capital-gains/deposit-securities); catch-all income recognition (condoned debts, excess provisions).
- **Topics:** taxation, payroll

## EVID-089 Ley ISR — no-renta y rentas no gravables (Art. 3-4)

- **Loc:** Art. 3 (pp.3-4), Art. 4 (pp.4-6).
- **Verbatim (thresholds):** viáticos/herramientas/uniformes/equipo not rent if "EN UNA CUANTÍA RAZONABLE" and necessary; must be backed "CON LOS DOCUMENTOS QUE ESTABLECEN LOS ARTÍCULOS 107 O 119 DEL CÓDIGO TRIBUTARIO" (Art. 3.1, p.3). Severance: "LAS INDEMNIZACIONES POR DESPIDO Y BONIFICACIONES POR RETIRO VOLUNTARIO, SIEMPRE QUE NO EXCEDAN DE UN SALARIO BÁSICO DE TREINTA DÍAS POR CADA AÑO DE SERVICIO. PARA ESTOS EFECTOS, NINGÚN SALARIO PODRÁ SER SUPERIOR AL SALARIO PROMEDIO DE LO DEVENGADO EN LOS ÚLTIMOS DOCE MESES, SIEMPRE Y CUANDO ESTOS SALARIOS HAYAN SIDO SUJETOS DE RETENCIÓN" (Art. 4.3, pp.4-5). Deposit interest exempt for natural persons if "EL SALDO PROMEDIO MENSUAL DE LOS DEPÓSITOS SEA INFERIOR A VEINTICINCO MIL DÓLARES (US$ 25,000.00)" (Art. 4.5, p.5). Insurance payouts not rent; endowment/race-free insurance ≤5y: "DEBERÁN PRACTICARSE LAS RETENCIONES CORRESPONDIENTES EN UN PORCENTAJE DEL 10% DE LAS SUMAS ACREDITADAS O PAGADAS" (Art. 4.7, p.5). First home sale: "EL VALOR DE TRANSACCIÓN NO SEA SUPERIOR A SETECIENTOS VEINTITRÉS SALARIOS MÍNIMOS" (Art. 4.12, p.6).
- **Gloss:** Art. 4 list: legislatively-declared non-taxables; diplomatic (reciprocity) but services in SV to foreign gov't/international organism ARE taxable unless treaty says otherwise (4.2); death/incapacity indemnities, jubilaciones/pensiones/montepíos exempt (4.3) but ordinary remunerations during sick leave are taxable; foreign-service Salvadoran officials' remunerations exempt (4.4); deposits (4.5); owner-occupied home imputed rent (4.6, max one home); insurance (4.7); Assembly/pubic-service prizes (4.9-4.10); certain foreign-credit interest from qualified lenders (4.11); fiscal lottery prizes per CT 118 (4.15). Donations/inheritances between ascendants/descendants 2nd degree & spouses not rent; substituted cost basis = antecesor's basis (Art. 3.2-3).
- **Candidate CRs:** payroll exemption engine (viáticos razonables, severance cap 30d/year × avg-12m salary, aguinaldo-type items); non-taxable income flags on deposit interest < $25k avg balance, first-home ≤723 SMM; 10% retention on short-term endowment payouts.
- **Topics:** taxation, payroll

## EVID-091 Ley ISR — ejercicio de imposición y métodos de cómputo

- **Loc:** Art. 13 (pp.8-9), Art. 17 (p.12), Art. 24 (p.14), Arts. 18-23 (pp.12-14), Art. 12 (p.8).
- **Gloss:** fiscal year = 12 months, Jan 1–Dec 31 for natural & juridical persons; liquidation on cessation/definitive departure; income presumed obtained at midnight of period end; each period independent "salvo las excepciones legales" (loss carryforward lives elsewhere — see EVID-106). Natural persons: CASH method (percibido, incl. availability), unless obligated to keep accounting → accrual; non-obligated may elect accrual (inform DGII in Nov-Dec of prior year; "ADOPTADO EL SISTEMA DE ACUMULACIÓN NO PODRÁ CAMBIARSE", Art. 17, p.12). Juridical persons: ACCRUAL (devengado; costs deductible when incurred though unpaid, subject to deductibility rules, Art. 24). Sucesiones: period splits at apertura/aceptación (Arts. 19-20); herederos include proportional succession income after acceptance (Art. 21); fideicomisos computed like natural persons, period from constitution to extinction (Art. 22); conjuntos: cash method, single subject (Art. 23). Usufructo legal de hijos to parents (Art. 18).
- **Candidate CRs:** fiscal-year calendar engine; cash-vs-accrual per taxpayer type with irrevocable accrual election for natural persons; period-split computation for estates/trusts.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting
- **Doubts/xref:** no FX/exchange-rate rule anywhere in this text (income in foreign currency) → check 04_Reglamento_ISR / CT (OQ-2).

## EVID-092 Ley ISR — territorialidad (rentas obtenidas en El Salvador)

- **Loc:** Art. 16 (pp.11-12), Art. 127 (p.55).
- **Verbatim:** "SE REPUTAN RENTAS OBTENIDAS EN EL SALVADOR, LAS QUE PROVENGAN DE BIENES SITUADOS EN EL PAÍS, ASÍ COMO DE ACTIVIDADES EFECTUADAS O DE CAPITALES INVERTIDOS EN EL MISMO, Y DE SERVICIOS PRESTADOS O UTILIZADOS EN EL TERRITORIO NACIONAL, AUNQUE SE RECIBAN O PAGUEN FUERA DE LA REPÚBLICA." (Art. 16, p.11). Services used in-country are SV-source to the provider regardless of where performed. Industrial/intellectual property registered or used in SV. Securities/derivatives: source if (a) emitter national/domiciled, (b) capital invested in-country, (c) risk located in-country — deemed located if obtainer is a resident or resident establishment. Government remunerations paid to Salvadoran officials abroad = SV-source. Final inciso: rents that are non-taxable/exempt abroad obtained by Salvadoran residents from credits/financing to foreign persons are deemed TAXABLE in SV (net result added to taxable base — anti-exemption rule).
- **Gloss:** Art. 127 apportionment for partially-in-country activities (absent treaties): gross SV income / total gross per activity = ratio applied to verified expenses → SV net income added to domestic net.
- **Candidate CRs:** source-rule classifier (goods/services/IP/securities/deposits); foreign-exempt income gross-up for residents; partial-territory apportionment worksheet.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-093 Ley ISR — ganancia de capital (Arts. 14, 42)

- **Loc:** Art. 14 (pp.9-10), Art. 42 (pp.37-38).
- **Verbatim:** "EL IMPUESTO SOBRE LA RENTA A PAGAR POR LA GANANCIA NETA DE CAPITAL DE UNA O VARIAS TRANSACCIONES... SERÁ EL EQUIVALENTE AL DIEZ POR CIENTO (10%) DE DICHAS GANANCIAS, SALVO CUANDO EL BIEN SE REALICE DENTRO DE LOS DOCE MESES SIGUIENTES A LA FECHA DE SU ADQUISICIÓN, EN CUYO CASO LA GANANCIA NETA DE CAPITAL DEBERÁ SUMARSE A LA RENTA NETA IMPONIBLE ORDINARIA" (Art. 42, p.37). Capital loss: deductible only against capital gains; excess "PODRÁ SER USADO DENTRO DE LOS CINCO AÑOS SIGUIENTES CONTRA FUTURAS GANANCIAS DE CAPITAL, SIEMPRE QUE SE DECLARE EN EL FORMULARIO QUE PARA TAL EFECTO PROPORCIONE LA ADMINISTRACIÓN TRIBUTARIA. EN NINGÚN CASO SERÁ DEDUCIBLE DE LA GANANCIA DE CAPITAL, LAS PÉRDIDAS DE CAPITAL PROVENIENTES DE OPERACIONES DISTINTAS" (Art. 14.2, p.9).
- **Gloss:** capital gain = gain of a person NOT habitually trading movable/immovable property; per-transaction result = transaction value − costo básico − conservación improvements − transaction expenses (already-deducted improvements excluded). Costo básico: acquisition cost − accumulated admitted depreciation; donated/inherited = transferor's basis. Banks/insurers/official credit institutions' extraordinary-asset liquidations = ORDINARY income. Art. 42 mechanics: >12 months → 10% separate, paid with annual declaration + calculation form; prior-year unused capital losses reduce current net gain. <12 months → ordinary income at progressive/30% rate.
- **Candidate CRs:** fixed-asset disposal engine (12-month rule → 10% vs ordinary; basis = cost − depreciation; improvements capitalization); 5-year capital-loss carryforward ledger restricted to capital-gain offset.
- **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-094 Ley ISR — títulos valores y depósitos financieros (rentas de capital al 10%)

- **Loc:** Art. 14-A (pp.10-11), Art. 27 (pp.14-15), Art. 15 (p.11, derogado), Art. 26 (p.14, derogado).
- **Verbatim:** títulos valores of domiciled natural persons "ESTARÁ GRAVADA CON EL IMPUESTO ESTABLECIDO EN ESTA LEY A UNA TASA DEL DIEZ POR CIENTO, LA CUAL SE LIQUIDARÁ SEPARADAMENTE DE LAS OTRAS RENTAS; SI A LAS REFERIDAS RENTAS SE LES EFECTUARON LAS RETENCIONES RESPECTIVAS, NO DEBERÁN DECLARARSE, CONSTITUYENDO LA RETENCIÓN EFECTUADA PAGO DEFINITIVO DEL IMPUESTO." (Art. 14-A, p.10). Deposits: natural persons "ESTÁN OBLIGADAS A PAGAR EL DIEZ POR CIENTO DEL IMPUESTO SOBRE LA RENTA; LA CUAL SE LIQUIDARÁ SEPARADAMENTE" — with CT Art. 159 retentions as definitive payment (Art. 27, p.14).
- **Gloss:** 10% separate-liquidation regime on securities income (dividends, interest, prizes, net capital gains); securities transfer result = price (≥ stock-exchange quote, else book value) − cost − expenses; cost by weighted average per species; losses only vs securities/other capital gains same period + 5 years, must be declared/registered. Foreign securities income of residents: same rules as Art. 27 deposits — foreign losses only vs foreign gains; top-up only (excess foreign tax NOT creditable/refundable); deposits foreign: rate <10% → pay difference; exempt abroad → add to taxable base; ≥ SV rate → declare as no sujetas. UIF informs DGII monthly (Lavado de Activos Art. 13). Natural-person non-resident-rate comparison rules for entities (Art. 27 last incisos).
- **Candidate CRs:** 10% final-withholding tax product for financial-institution income; weighted-average cost for securities; foreign top-up computation (cap at SV rate, no refund of excess).
- **Topics:** taxation
- **Doubts/xref:** tension Art. 27 (10% on ALL deposit interest) vs Art. 4.5 (exempt if avg balance < US$25,000) vs CT 159 rate — reconcile via CT pass (OQ-6). Art. 14-A cites repealed Art. 4.13 (old dividend exemption) — stale cross-ref (OQ-5).

## EVID-097 Ley ISR — renta neta y proporcionalidad de deducciones (Art. 28)

- **Loc:** Art. 28 (p.16).
- **Gloss:** renta neta = renta obtenida − costs/expenses necessary for production of income & conservation of source "QUE ESTA LEY DETERMINE" plus legal deductions; deductibility requirements of this law AND CT must all be met. Costs related to non-taxable/non-renta income NEVER deductible. - **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-098 Ley ISR — deducciones generales del negocio (Art. 29)

- **Loc:** Art. 29 (pp.16-21).
- **Gloss (numerals):** 1) business expenses (fletes, propaganda, libros, energía, teléfono...) — NOT client/employee perks (boleos aéreos, cuotas de clubes, joyas); 2) remunerations for services directly in production of gravada renta, CONDITIONED on having effected & entered social-security and ISR retentions; same for severance (per Art. 4.3) — payments to relatives within 4th degree/2nd affinity/spouse/companeros require proof the work was necessary & actually performed (also for legal reps, directors, advisers, shareholders); 3) foreign travel (pasajes + taxes/port dues + documented food/lodging strictly business-linked; traveller must be taxpayer/legal rep/employee with proven labor link) + domestic viáticos per Art. 3.1; 4) leases of goods used directly in production, pro-rated by time used in gravadas (except seasonal); 5) insurance premiums on income-producing property (natural persons: only 50% of home-insurance premium if home partially used as business office); 6) taxes/rates/special contributions on imports, services or the income source — caused & paid in the period (December: caused + paid within legal deadline), EXCLUDING ISR, IVA (subject to IVA-law Art. 70), transfer of real estate tax, fines/surcharges/interest; 7) salaried fixed deduction (→ EVID-096); 8) fuel for machinery/cargo-transport/work equipment (not cost per numeral 11), delivery/collective-transport/salespersons' vehicles, activo realizable vehicles — documented with factura/CCF a nombre; 9) ordinary repairs/maintenance (no remodeling/value-increase/life-extension); 10) loan interest invested in the gravada source + loan constitution/renewal/cancellation costs pro-rated over financing term; NOT deductible: interest on own capital/utilities not owed to third parties; interest linked to non-gravable assets (capitalize into asset cost, deduct via depreciation); "interest" on preferred shares = dividends; back-to-back lower-rate rule (deduct source interest only up to the lower on-lent rate); 11) COGS: opening inventory + production/acquisition costs − closing inventory; valuation method per CT Art. 143; inventories must match CT Art. 142 registry — "NO SERÁN DEDUCIBLES... LAS DIFERENCIAS EN EL COSTO DE VENTAS, CUANDO SE INCUMPLAN" (p.20); production cost = raw material + labor + indirect fabrication costs, deductible only as finished goods sold; 12) agropecuario indispensable expenses (own-harvest products & own labor excluded). Final: manufacturing costs accumulated to prorrata via costing systems, deducted as products sold.
- **Candidate CRs:** payroll-expense deductibility gate (retentions effected & entered); relative-payment proof rule; 50% home-office insurance cap; fuel whitelist by vehicle use; repair-vs-improvement capitalization test; interest allocation & capitalization rules; inventory-based COGS tied to CT 142/143 registries.
- **Topics:** taxation, payroll

## EVID-099 Ley ISR — otras deducciones: reserva legal, incobrables, donaciones (Arts. 31-32)

Donations to Art. 6 entities "HASTA UN LÍMITE MÁXIMO DEL VEINTE POR CIENTO DEL VALOR RESULTANTE DE RESTAR A LA RENTA NETA DEL DONANTE EN EL PERIODO O EJERCICIO DE IMPOSICIÓN RESPECTIVO, EL VALOR DE LA DONACIÓN" (Art. 32.4, p.33); in-kind donations valued at donor cost (depreciated assets: cost − depreciation); must be free & irrevocable; donee previously qualified as excluded by AT; documents with AT-authorized numbering; NOT deductible if beneficiary is donor/family (4th degree/spouse) or, for entities, socios/directivos/representatives/their families.

## EVID-100 Ley ISR — costos y gastos NO deducibles (Art. 29-A)

- **Loc:** Art. 29-A (pp.21-26), Art. 126 (p.55).
- **Verbatim (key limits):** retention-linked: costs related to retention-subject rents not deductible if retention unpaid (12-13, incl. December rule via Art. 62 inc. 2). Interest/commissions/financial-insurance payments NON-deductible when: (a) retentions not effected (ISR or IVA) where due; (b) related domiciled lender didn't declare them as gravada renta; (c) "SE EXCEDIERE AL RESULTADO DE APLICAR A LOS PRÉSTAMOS O CRÉDITOS, LA TASA PROMEDIO DE INTERÉS ACTIVA SOBRE CRÉDITOS MÁS CUATRO PUNTOS ADICIONALES" with related/foreign preferential-regime lender; (d) related/paraíso lender when debt "EXCEDA DEL RESULTADO DE MULTIPLICAR POR TRES VECES EL VALOR DEL PATRIMONIO O CAPITAL CONTABLE PROMEDIO" (thin-cap 3:1; average equity = (start+end)/2; financial-supervised entities & savings-credit coops exempt from these rules) (num. 14, pp.22-23). IVA-control documents non-deductible when emitter unregistered, operation unproven, numbering not AT-assigned, document not in acquirer's name or economic burden not shown (num. 15). Undocumented/unrecorded costs (18) with closing figures definitive unless CPA-certified (auditor certification per CT 134 context). Goodwill/marks/intangibles amortization (19). Fines/penalties/interest moratorio (20, except regulator-mandated compensations on declared income). Deteriorated/obsolete inventory losses (21) & accounting provisions not expressly allowed (22) non-deductible. **"LAS ADQUISICIONES DE BIENES O UTILIZACIÓN DE SERVICIOS, CUYOS MONTOS SEAN IGUALES O MAYORES A VEINTICINCO SALARIOS MÍNIMOS MENSUALES QUE: i) NO SE REALICEN POR MEDIO DE CHEQUE, TRANSFERENCIA BANCARIA, TARJETAS DE CRÉDITO O DÉBITO. ii) EL MEDIO DE PAGO SEA DIFERENTE AL EFECTIVO Y A LOS MEDIOS UTILIZADOS EN EL ROMANO (i), Y NO SE FORMALIZASEN EN CONTRATO ESCRITO..."** (num. 23, p.25) — cash-payment disallowance ≥ 25 SMM (compare IVA 58-SMM rule, EVID-050). Capital losses (Art. 14/42 transactions and others) never deductible from ordinary income (10); losses from related-subject or preferential-regime transactions never deductible. Personal/living costs, non-business remunerations, capital yields to partners (unless proven invested), unproven travel, capital-asset acquisitions, personal vehicles/housing/fuel, retained profits, preferred dividends, non-qualifying donations, catch-all non-indispensable (1-11, 16, 24).
- **Gloss:** Art. 126 states the non-deduction lists merely incorporate prior rules (interpretive).

## EVID-103 Ley ISR — impuesto a la distribución de utilidades 5% (Arts. 72-74-C, 25)

- **Loc:** Arts. 72-73 (p.43), Art. 74 (p.44), Art. 74-A (pp.44-45), Art. 74-B (p.45), Art. 74-C (pp.45-46), Art. 25 (p.14).
- **Verbatim:** "LOS SUJETOS PASIVOS DOMICILIADOS QUE PAGUEN O ACREDITEN UTILIDADES A SUS SOCIOS, ACCIONISTAS... ESTARÁN OBLIGADOS A RETENER UN PORCENTAJE DEL CINCO POR CIENTO (5%) DE TALES SUMAS. DICHA RETENCIÓN CONSTITUIRÁ PAGO DEFINITIVO DEL IMPUESTO SOBRE LA RENTA A CARGO DEL SUJETO AL QUE SE LE REALIZÓ LA RETENCIÓN, SEA ÉSTE DOMICILIADO O NO. SI A LAS REFERIDAS UTILIDADES NO SE LES EFECTUARON LAS RETENCIONES... SE DEBERÁ DECLARAR SEPARADAMENTE... Y PAGAR EL IMPUESTO A LA TASA DEL CINCO POR CIENTO (5%)." (Art. 72, p.43). "Utilidades" = gravadas+exentas+no sujetas rents − costs, expenses, deductions and the ISR of Arts. 37/41; paid/credited when actually received (cash, títulos, in-kind, debt compensation, loss application, accounting operations) — "TALES COMO DIVIDENDOS, PARTICIPACIONES SOCIALES, EXCEDENTES, RESULTADOS, RESERVA LEGAL, GANANCIAS O RENDIMIENTOS". Retention prevails over contrary norms "SALVO LO REGULADO EN EL ARTÍCULO 158-A DEL CÓDIGO TRIBUTARIO".
- **Gloss:** permanent establishments paying/crediting profits to non-domiciliaries retain 5% (Art. 73; unretained → Art. 53 inc. 2 self-declaration). Capital reductions: 5% on the portion from capitalized/reinvested earnings, deemed profits-first (Art. 74). Loans/mutuos/advances/any financing by domiciled entities to (a) partners & their related subjects (per Art. 25 final), (b) preferential-regime/paraíso entities, (c) foreign head office/related foreign establishments → 5% retention, EXCEPT: market-or-better interest, regulated financial institutions, habitual credit entities, inter-institutional contracts, or borrower is State/municipality/autonomous/public-utility corporation; if borrower defaults >6 installments or term >1 year → total consideration = gravable renta and interest non-deductible (Art. 74-A). Art. 25: society loans to shareholders/family (4th degree/2nd affinity, spouse) = distributed dividends / gravable renta unless lender is bank/credit entity. No-retention cases (Art. 74-B): profits already taxed on prior distributions; capitalization in nominative shares/participaciones of same society; reinvestment by entes sin personalidad jurídica; recipient is State/municipality/public entity/coop federations/qualified public-utility corporations; autonomous official institutions ARE subject despite their exemption laws; exemption must be proven to DGII. Art. 74-C: per-exercise Registro de Control de Utilidades (determination, paid/credited, capitalization/reinvestment, capital reductions, per each socio/accionista/partícipe/beneficiario, consistent with accounting); DGII issues administrative norms; breach sanctioned via CT Art. 242 b)/c)1).
- **Candidate CRs:** 5% dividend-withholding tax (definitive, domiciled or not) on any deemed-distribution event incl. partner loans & capital reductions; earnings-register (EDA-style) tracking per-shareholder taxed vs untaxed profits; exception validator (market-rate, institutions, reinvestment).
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting
- **Doubts/xref:** CT Art. 158-A carve-out + CT 242 sanction — collect in CT pass; register format norms may exist as DGII resolution (OQ-3 adjacent).
