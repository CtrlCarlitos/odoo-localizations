## EVID-300 — NIIF 18 Presentación y Revelación (issued Apr-2024, effective 2027-01-01): 5 income categories + MPM disclosures; NIC 7 changes

- **Loc:** NIIF 18 chapter (txt PAGE 162-169).
- **Verbatim:** "El 9 de abril de 2024, el IASB emitió la NIIF 18 Presentación e Información a Revelar en los Estados Financieros, que reemplaza a la NIC 1." Five categories: "actividades de operación, inversión, financiación, impuesto a las ganancias y operaciones discontinuadas" with subtotales for "resultados operativos", "resultados antes de financiación e impuesto a las ganancias" and "resultados"; principal-economic-activity assessments (investing/financing-to-customers entities get specific routing); financing category = Type-1 (pure financing) liabilities initial+subsequent measurement ± incremental issuance/settlement costs; operations = residual category ("incluirá todos los ingresos y gastos… incluso si dichos ingresos y gastos son volátiles o inusuales"); equity-method results → investing category; **management-defined performance measures (MPM)** = "un subtotal de los ingresos y los gastos que una entidad utiliza en comunicaciones públicas fuera de los estados financieros" — single-note disclosure incl. calculation + reconciliation (gross profit and similar NOT MPMs); aggregation/disaggregation by shared/different characteristics with meaningful labels; **NIC 7 changes: indirect-method starting point becomes "resultados operativos"; interest/dividend classification options REMOVED — "los dividendos abonados se incluirán en los flujos de efectivo de financiación"; non-specific entities: interest paid → financing, interest/dividends received → investing**; effective 2027-01-01, early adoption permitted (disclose), retrospective + prior-year P&L reconciliation.
- **Gloss:** the 2027 presentation regime — **for FULL NIIF entities; NIIF PYMES (32_) is NOT amended by NIIF 18** (PYMES keeps Sec 5 nature/function + Sec 7 interest/dividend choices). Cross-framework trap: a PYMES reporter must NOT adopt the 5-category split; a full-NIIF group member loses the interest/dividend choices in 2027. Version-regime watch item (D12): full-NIIF group entities' 2027 cutover.
- **Candidate CRs:** none for PYMES-default SV scope (watch note only); full-NIIF group entities → external reporting concern, out of Odoo-localization core (recorded as boundary).
- **Topics:** chart-of-accounts (version-watch).
- **Doubts/xref:** 32_ EVID-277/279/280 (PYMES presentation rules unaffected); D12 version regime.

## EVID-301 — NIIF 19 Subsidiaries without Public Accountability (effective 2027-01-01): reduced disclosures on full-NIIF recognition

- **Loc:** NIIF 19 chapter (txt PAGE 170-175).
- **Verbatim:** "permite a las entidades elegibles optar por aplicar los requisitos de información a revelar reducidos de la NIIF 19 sin dejar de aplicar los requisitos de reconocimiento, medición y presentación de otras normas contables NIIF." Eligibility: subsidiary + no public accountability + parent (ultimate or intermediate) produces publicly-available IFRS-compliant consolidated FS. Public accountability uses "el mismo lenguaje que la norma contable NIIF para PYMES". Disclosure principles built FROM the PYMES standard's disclosure requirements ("los requisitos de información a revelar de la norma contable NIIF para las PYMES se han utilizado en la NIIF 19" where recognition/measurement coincide; adapted where they differ). Excludes NIIF 8 segments, NIIF 17, NIC 33 from the reduction. Voluntary, revocable, re-electable; explicit compliance declaration; election change ≠ NIIF 1/NIC 8 policy change. Effective 2027-01-01, early adoption permitted. Rationale printed: PYMES adoption by group subsidiaries "exige que una subsidiaria que aplica la norma contable NIIF para las PYMES mantenga registros contables adicionales" — NIIF 19 removes that duplicate-books burden.
- **Gloss:** the third SV-relevant framework path: (a) NIIF PYMES standalone; (b) full NIIF; (c) **full NIIF + NIIF 19 reduced disclosures** (group subsidiaries, 2027+). For Odoo: recognition/measurement identical to full NIIF — only the notes package shrinks. Version-watch + boundary note.
- **Candidate CRs:** none for core (watch note); the framework-flag concept from EVID-299 gains a third value (niif_plenas + niif19_disclosures from 2027, informational).
- **Topics:** chart-of-accounts (version-watch).
- **Doubts/xref:** 32_ OQ-1 (which SV entities land where = SV adopting instrument gap).

## EVID-302 — NIIF S1/S2 sustainability standards + non-financial-report landscape: scope boundary for the localization

- **Loc:** NIIF S1/S2 chapter (txt PAGE 196-211) + Reportes no Financieros (txt PAGE 176-195) + FS-climate connection (txt PAGE 212-227).
- **Verbatim:** S1 (general requirements) + S2 (climate) issued 26-jun-2023, "entran en vigencia para los periodos anuales que comiencen a partir del 1 de enero del 2024", jurisdiction-dependent adoption: "La aplicación… depende de la legislación de cada jurisdicción o procesos regulatorios. La aplicación… no está vinculada a la aplicación de las… (NIIF)." Four pillars (gobernanza/estrategia/gestión de riesgo/métricas y objetivos) from TCFD; materiality aligned to IFRS; S2 GHG Scope 1-2-3 disclosures per GHG Protocol (tonnes CO2e; scope-3 one-year transition exemption; "primero el clima" first-year option; comparative exemption year 1); proportionality principle (reasonable-and-supportable info without disproportionate cost — same disproportionate-cost idiom as PYMES 2.28). Landscape: SEC climate rule (US), ESRS/CSRD (EU, double materiality), ISSB (investor materiality); climate-FS connection examples (NIC 16 useful-life/residual/dismantling effects; NIC 37 net-zero constructive-obligation analysis — commitments ≠ present obligation until resources received/emissions occur; NIIF 9 ECL climate scenarios).
- **Gloss:** sustainability disclosure = a distinct reporting layer from the financial statements; **no SV adoption instrument for NIIF S1/S2 exists in the corpus** (jurisdiction-dependent; El Salvador appears only as an ESG-survey data point, not an adopting jurisdiction). Boundary ruling for the wave: Odoo localization core = financial-statement engine; S1/S2 disclosure data (emissions inventories etc.) is out of scope, recorded as boundary + watch. The connection examples double as PYMES-relevant judgment areas (useful life, provisions, impairment indicators — all exist in 32_ Secs 17/21/27).
- **Candidate CRs:** none (boundary note: emissions/sustainability KPI tracking out of localization scope; no invented SV obligations — SAS discipline).
- **Topics:** chart-of-accounts (scope boundary).
- **Doubts/xref:** none.

- **Verbatim:** NIC 1 current/non-current liability classification + covenants (effective 2024-01-01: right-to-defer must exist at period end; classification unaffected by exercise probability/management intent; settlement-via-equity counts unless conversion option itself is equity; rollover ≠ settlement; disclosure when non-current classification rests on future covenants); NIIF 16 sale-leaseback lease-liability measurement (2024-01-01: no gain/loss on retained right-of-use; seller-lessee determines "lease payments" so no retained-ROU gain is recognized); NIIF 10/NIC 28 investor-to-associate/JV asset sales (deferred indefinitely, early adoption allowed; full gain when a business is transferred, otherwise gain limited to unrelated investors' interests); **supplier-finance disclosures NIC 7 + NIIF 7 (2024-01-01)** — terms/conditions, carrying amounts + financier-paid amounts unless impracticable, maturity ranges vs comparable trade payables, non-cash change types (the same regime PYMES 3rd ed. imported as 7.19B-C, 32_ EVID-280 — convergent proof); NIC 12 Pillar Two (mandatory deferred-tax exception + separate current-expense disclosure — PYMES 29.3A mirror, 32_ EVID-296); **NIC 21 lack of convertibility (2025-01-01)** — convertibility assessment framework + estimated spot rate (the same framework PYMES 3rd ed. imported as 30.5A + Apéndice 30A, 32_ EVID-297); NIIF 9/7 classification-and-measurement amendments (2026-01-01: electronic-settlement derecognition policy option; ESG-linked cash-flow characteristics assessment; non-recourse assets; disclosure additions).
