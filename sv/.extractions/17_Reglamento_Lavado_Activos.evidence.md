## EVID-232 — reglamento clock/threshold block is COLONES-ERA and superseded

- **Loc:** Art. 3 (p.1-2).
- **Verbatim:** "El plazo de tres días hábiles estipulado en el Art. 9 de la Ley... se computará a partir del día siguiente en que se compruebe que la operación o la transacción múltiple ha excedido del monto de Quinientos Mil Colones o su equivalente en moneda extranjera. Para los efectos de aplicación del inciso 1º del Art. 9 de la Ley, el plazo de un mes que en el mismo se señala, habrá de computarse como los anteriores treinta días continuos, es decir, contados a partir del momento en que se realizó la última transacción, entendiéndose que si en dos o más operaciones el monto de éstas exceden a los quinientos mil colones... las Instituciones estarán en la obligación de informar a la Unidad de Investigación Financiera... al existir suficientes elementos de juicio para considerar que tales operaciones o transacciones son irregulares o sospechosas; o bien, cuando lo requiera la UIF."
- **Gloss:** superseded values (¢500,000 ≈ $57,143 at 8.75 parity — never the current $10,000; 3 días hábiles ≠ current 5) BUT the **window mechanics survive as the only corpus definition of the Art. 9 "término de un mes": 30 continuous days counted BACK from the last transaction**, with early-report duty once the cumulative multiple-operation total crosses the threshold before the window closes. Implement the window anchor from here, the amounts/clock from 15_ Art. 9.
- **Candidate CRs:** rolling-window definition for the per-client cash aggregator (30 continuous days back from latest transaction; early trigger on threshold crossing).
- **Topics:** commercial-legal (core).
- **Doubts/xref:** none.

## EVID-233 — institutional obligations program + confidentiality (no tip-off) + liaison designation

- **Loc:** Arts. 4, 5, 11 (p.2-3).
- **Verbatim:** Art. 4: institutions shall... b) "adoptar, desarrollar y ejecutar programas, normas, procedimientos y controles internos... para prevenir y detectar las actividades relacionadas con el delito"; c) client-knowledge policy; d) personnel-integrity procedures + internal audit; e) permanent training programs "tanto en lo relativo a procesos y técnicas... como en la forma de reportar oportunamente... en forma razonada y documentada"; f) analyze internal reports and inform UIF + Superintendencia immediately on irregular/suspicious findings; g) "Comunicar a la UIF y a los organismos de fiscalización o supervisión, en un plazo de quince días hábiles, la designación o cambio de los funcionarios" (program executors + supervisors acting as UIF liaison); h) resourcing/authority for those officers; i) communication channels with UIF; j) "Guardar confidencialidad de toda información transmitida o requerida... de manera que no podrán divulgarla a ninguna persona, incluso a los usuarios o clientes investigados, salvo por orden de un juez competente o del Fiscal General de la República." Art. 11: institutions must follow UIF instructions and report compliance/results + formularios issued by the FGR.
- **Gloss:** the compliance-program skeleton: internal controls, training, internal report → analysis → immediate UIF notification; **15-días-hábiles duty to notify UIF of compliance-liaison designation/change**; explicit NO-TIP-OFF rule (confidentiality extends to the investigated client). FGR issues the operative forms.
- **Candidate CRs:** compliance-liaison registry event (designation/change → 15-días-hábiles notification clock); report-object confidentiality invariant (never client-visible); training/audit program trackers (lightweight).
- **Topics:** commercial-legal.
- **Doubts/xref:** none.

## EVID-234 — suspicious-transaction indicator catalogs (red flags)

- **Loc:** Arts. 12–18 (p.3-5).
- **Verbatim:** Art. 12: irregular/suspicious = "todas las operaciones poco usuales, las que se encuentran fuera de los patrones de transacción habituales y las que no sean significativas pero sí periódicas, sin fundamento económico o legal evidentes, y todas aquellas operaciones inconsistentes o que no guardan relación con el tipo de actividad económica del cliente." Art. 13: special attention to a) day-to-day/inhábile-hour multiple transfers; b) early/oversized loan repayments without explained origin; c) international monetary instruments disproportionate to activity. Art. 14: markedly unusual operations (CD-collateralized loans; safe-box visits followed by just-under-threshold cash deposits; cashier's/traveler's cheque purchases in big cash or just under reportable amounts — "justo bajo el monto requerido para generar un informe"; cash-heavy fideicomisos; casa-de-cambio structured deposits; constant ATM deposits; marked instruments). Art. 15: evasive conduct (refusing form info; pressing staff not to keep transaction reports). Art. 16: transfer patterns (sub-threshold deposits consolidated then sent abroad; round-trip transfers; no-currency-change international transfers; received-transfers immediately converted into instruments for third-party payments). Art. 17: insufficient/suspicious KYC info (incomplete purpose/directors; refusing personal background above limits; account without references/address/ID; suspect/false documents; no employment history; refusing financial statements; statements notably different from similar businesses; false/inexact information). Art. 18: pattern changes (correspondent cash patterns; cash growth without reported-transaction growth; high-denomination movement disproportionate to location; small-denomination surges; unjustified deposit frequency/volume increases).
- **Gloss:** the red-flag taxonomy — the detection-rule menu an Odoo AML monitor would configure: structuring (just-under-threshold), velocity/aggregation, activity-consistency, KYC-refusal, pattern-shift. Lawful basis for configurable indicator rules; values all qualitative (no hard numbers beyond "justo bajo el monto").
- **Candidate CRs:** configurable red-flag rule engine seeded with the Art. 13–18 catalogs (structuring detection vs current threshold; window-velocity; activity-profile deviation; KYC-completeness); case file with documented analysis (Art. 4 e/f) feeding Art. 9-A clocks (15_).
- **Topics:** commercial-legal (core).
- **Doubts/xref:** indicator rules are advisory-detection (law requires reporting judgment, not automated reporting) — FRs should frame engine output as case-creation, not auto-report.

## EVID-235 — unsupervised subjects + border declaration mechanics

- **Loc:** Arts. 19–22 (p.5-6).
- **Verbatim:** Art. 19: natural/juridical persons in activities under the law's control "que no estén fiscalizadas o supervisadas por un organismo específico, deberán comunicar a la UIF las operaciones... de manera reiterada y en dinero en efectivo, cuando exceda de la suma prevista en el Art. 9 de la Ley, o sobre transacciones... que por el monto, número, complejidad, características o circunstancias especiales, se alejaren de los patrones habituales" — using the FGR form, "plazo de tres días hábiles" per Art. 3 mechanics [stale clock — 15_ Art. 9's 5 días hábiles governs post-reform]. Art. 20: PNC vehicle/person searches with dignity safeguards (same-sex agents). Art. 21: entry declaration via FGR form at migration posts; forms remitted to UIF when indicia of untruth or on UIF request. Art. 22: FGR verifies; falsity/omission/inexactitude with legitimate origin → "multa del cinco por ciento del monto total del valor retenido"; if legality not demonstrated within 30 days of retention → decomiso.
- **Gloss:** unsupervised subjects report through the same FGR/UIF form channel (their unsupervised status only waives the Oficialía — 15_ Art. 2 tail); border-declaration workflow (form, retention, 5% multa, 30-day decomiso clock) is state-side context.
- **Candidate CRs:** unsupervised-subject reporting profile (same thresholds, no compliance office).
- **Topics:** commercial-legal.
- **Doubts/xref:** the 3-day clock here is stale (supersession note above); 30-day decomiso window is state-side, informational only.
