## EVID-130 Reglamento ISR — "renta obtenida" concept; cash vs. accrual computation

- **Loc:** Arts. 9, 10 (pp.4-5).
- **Verbatim:** none threshold-bearing; Art. 10 defines when income is "percibido": "ya sea en efectivo, en especies o en servicios. Aunque los ingresos no hubiesen sido cobrados en efectivo o en especie, se considera que el contribuyente los ha percibido, siempre que hayan estado disponibles, o cuando han sido reinvertidos, acumulados, capitalizados, acreditados en cuenta, puestos en reserva..." (Art. 10, p.4)
- **Gloss:** Art. 9: renta obtenida = ALL income percibido o devengado, gross, in money or kind, incl. (a) personal-service remuneration (salaries, dietas, aguinaldos, commissions, pensions...), (b) professional/arts/sports fees, prizes, royalties, (c) business activity profits, (d) use/exploitation of property (rents, interest, dividends, royalties, llave, marks, patents), (e) any commercialization transactions, (f) catch-all; even amounts later excluded/non-taxable are "renta obtenida" first. Art. 10: cash method = income when actually received (constructive receipt: available, reinvested, capitalized, credited, reserved) and expenses when paid; accrual method = devengadas/incurridos regardless of collection.
- **Candidate CRs:** Odoo ISR computation base = gross revenue recognition model with constructive-receipt rules; income-category taxonomy for ISR mapping.
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting
- **Doubts/xref:** Ties to Ley ISR Art. 13 (computation systems, 03_). No repeal marker — substantive, survives.

## EVID-132 Reglamento ISR — installment sales deferral, repossession & in-kind rent

- **Loc:** Arts. 12, 13, 14 (pp.5-6).
- **Verbatim:** "podrán ser diferidas, si han sido obtenidas en operación de crédito cuyos plazos sean mayores a veinticuatro meses, en la parte proporcional que corresponda al valor de las cuotas pendientes de cobro, siempre que medie contrato celebrado en forma legal." (Art. 12, p.5)
- **Gloss:** Art. 12: habitual traders (compraventa/permuta of movable/immovable goods) may DEFER profit on credit sales with terms >24 months, proportionally to uncollected instalments; deferred amounts flow into subsequent years' net income proportionally to quotas collected/accrued; interest on balances = income when received/accrued; rent-with-promise-to-sell: cánones are income when realized (interest same rule); final inciso (added by reform (2)): these deferral rules do NOT apply to capital gains. Art. 13: repossession → gain/loss of the repossession year = real value of recovered asset − (unsatisfied quotas + rescates pagados). Art. 14: rents compensated in products/fruits/improvements valued at market price at fiscal-year-end.
- **Candidate CRs:** deferred-revenue/profit engine for >24-month instalment contracts; repossession gain/loss posting; in-kind rent valuation.
- **Topics:** taxation
- **Doubts/xref:** Interaction with current Ley Art. 14/14-A/42 capital-gain regime (03_: Art. 42 = 10% on ganancia neta de capital) — carve-out wording is from 1993 reform; verify no later supersession (OQ-4). Survives otherwise.

## EVID-133 Reglamento ISR — habitualidad definition & presumptions

- **Loc:** Art. 15 (p.6).
- **Gloss:** Operates Ley Art. 14 inc. 1 (habitual traders): habituality = acts forming the corporate object/giro with intent to engage in the negotiated dealings, merchant or not. - **Topics:** taxation

## EVID-134 Reglamento ISR — capital gain: cost basis & years-of-possession counting

- **Loc:** Art. 16 (pp.6-7); INCISO QUINTO SUPRIMIDO (2) (p.7).
- **Gloss:** Pre-1992 assets: cost basis = value declared for the patrimonio tax at 31-dic-1990 (or special-year-end); donation/inheritance → carryover donor's basis per same rules; un-declared assets → last valuation declared before 31-dic-1990; NO revaluation allowed at 31-dic-1990. Year-counting: (a) acquired in H1 → whole year; (b) transferred in H2 → whole year; (c) acquired and transferred within the following 6 months → whole year in the enajenación year. Transaction value = contract price; rent-with-promise-to-sell = total rent price + sale price if not included. Fifth inciso suppressed by reform (2).
- **Candidate CRs:** asset holding-period computation for SV capital-gain disposal logic (holding-period field on fixed assets).
- **Topics:** taxation
- **Doubts/xref:** Pre-2001-transition historical rule (colones-era patrimonio valuations) — mostly legacy; keep for completeness. Feeds Art. 17 method (EVID-135).

## EVID-135 Reglamento ISR — capital gain: 9-step media-tasa computation (Art. 17)

- **Loc:** Art. 17 (p.7; final ¶ p.8).
- **Verbatim:** "aplicando la tabla del Art. 37, la tasa del Art. 41 o la tasa del 25% según sea, persona natural domiciliada, persona jurídica domiciliada o no, sucesión o fideicomiso no domiciliados, respectivamente" (Art. 17 n.6, p.7) · "el cual deberá dividirse entre 2, para obtener la media tasa" (Art. 17 n.7, p.7) · "se aplicará sobre la totalidad constituida por la renta imponible ordinaria más la ganancia de capital, como tasa media efectiva, el 1.0%, cuyo resultado será el impuesto a pagar" (Art. 17 final ¶, p.8)
- **Gloss:** Procedure for assets held a whole number of years: (1) ordinary-course taxable income; (2) capital gain/loss = transaction value − cost basis − value-preserving improvements − transaction expenses; (3) count years (H1-acquisition / H2-transfer count as full years, + intermediate full years); (4) renta del período = capital gain ÷ years possessed; (5) add to ordinary taxable income; (6) tax on total per Ley Art. 37 table (natural persons) / Art. 41 rate (juridical) / 25% (non-domiciled succession/trust); (7) coefficient = that tax ÷ income of step 5, ÷2 = media tasa; (8) media tasa × remaining gain (the part not previously incorporated) added to step-6 tax; (9) subtract retentions, anticipos/pagos a cuenta, and Ley Art. 27 credits. Fallback (final ¶): if ordinary income + renta del período does not reach a taxable base in full, apply 1.0% effective average rate on (ordinary taxable income + entire capital gain).
- **Candidate CRs:** capital-gain ISR computation routine (multi-year averaging + half-rate + 1.0% fallback) for asset-disposal posting.
- **Topics:** taxation
- **Doubts/xref:** Rate references are STALE vs current Ley (03_): Art. 41 now 30% (>US$150,000) / 25% (≤US$150,000), and Ley Art. 42 sets flat 10% on ganancia neta de capital — whether Art. 17's scheme still governs any case (assets held whole years by persons?) must be checked against current 03_ Arts. 14/42 (OQ-2).

## EVID-137 Reglamento ISR — specific income inclusions: real rights, interest presumption, non-compete, life annuity, insurers

- **Loc:** Arts. 22, 23, 24, 25, 26 (pp.8-9).
- **Verbatim:** "la diferencia entre la cantidad que reciba el deudor y la mayor cantidad que devuelva, se considerará interés. Asimismo, se presume, salvo prueba en contrario, que todo crédito devenga el interés legal" (Art. 23, p.8)
- **Gloss:** Art. 22: computable income from constituting usufruct/use/habitation/servidumbre in favor of third parties, guarantees of obligations, and any consideration for ceding use/enjoyment of movable or immovable property. Art. 23: interest = excess returned over received; every credit presumed to yield legal (civil/commercial-code) interest. Art. 24: payments received for non-compete (obligaciones de no hacer) or abandoning an activity = taxable. Art. 25: life annuity (onerosa): annual legal-interest fraction of the price is taxable income, remainder = price recovery; algorithm a)-d) (first year: price × legal annual interest rate; subtract from amounts paid → recovery; next-year base = price − prior recoveries, until exhaustion); after full recovery, everything taxable; death of beneficiary before term → unrecovered balance = debtor's taxable gain. Art. 26: insurer income composition (premiums collected, investment income, reinsurer/reafianzador collections, indemnity/share commissions, other profits).
- **Candidate CRs:** income-recognition rules for annuity/interest-imputation products (niche); mostly awareness-level.
- **Topics:** taxation, special-regimes
- **Doubts/xref:** "interés legal" rate NOT defined in this document (Código Civil/Comercio source, outside corpus) — OQ-7.

## EVID-139 Reglamento ISR — exclusions from renta: work tools & viáticos; bank-deposit interest; casa de habitación

- **Loc:** Arts. 28, 29, 30 (p.10).
- **Verbatim:** "viáticos para transporte, alimentación y estadía en una cuantía razonable, herramientas de trabajo, equipo de oficina entregado y cualquier otro valor o bien de igual significado que le permitan al tarbajador desempeñar mejor su trabajo" (Art. 28, p.10; typo "tarbajador" as printed)
- **Gloss:** Art. 28 (implements Ley Art. 3 Nº 1): employer deliveries to workers that do not directly benefit the worker or increase his patrimony are NOT renta obtenida — reasonable travel per-diem (transport/food/lodging), tools, office equipment; burden on the EMPLOYER to prove via documents/registries that the money/goods were used for the assigned work. Art. 29: bank/financial-institution deposit interest, prizes and utilities NOT computable — but only if beneficiary is a NATURAL PERSON and direct owner/depositor. Art. 30 (implements Ley Art. 4 Nº 6): definitions of casa de habitación (owned or free-usufruct, inhabited with spouse/ascendants/descendants/siblings; gratuitous real-right use must impute market rent as taxable) and quinta/recreo house (seasonal); max ONE of each per taxpayer.
- **Candidate CRs:** payroll viático/expense-reimbursement non-taxable handling with employer documentation duty; one-house rule for personal deductions.
- **Topics:** taxation, payroll
- **Doubts/xref:** Art. 29 is NARROWER than reformed Ley Art. 4 Nº 5 (03_: any subject, supervised institutions incl. federations/cooperatives) — law governs; Reglamento text stale (OQ-3). Art. 28 documentation duty survives alongside Ley Art. 3 (verify current numbering in 03_).

## EVID-140 Reglamento ISR — renta neta: necessary costs, formal accounting & gravada/no-gravable segregation

- **Loc:** Arts. 31, 32 (p.11).
- **Verbatim:** "se distribuirán por iguales partes entre la actividad gravable y la no gravable, de tal manera que dichas erogaciones afecten ambas actividades." (Art. 32, p.11)
- **Gloss:** Art. 31: net taxable income = renta obtenida − necessary costs/expenses for producing income and conserving its source; conservation expense = keeping the source in working condition WITHOUT value increase; production costs = indispensable outlays before earning the income (not post-earning employment of it). Art. 32: formally-accounting taxpayers: start from book net income of P&L accounts, add back non-deductible items and any untaxed income; MUST segregate gravable vs. non-gravable activity income/outflows so non-gravable results (positive or negative) do NOT affect gravable results; non-attributable common expenses split 50/50 ("por iguales partes") between gravable and non-gravable.
- **Candidate CRs:** Odoo P&L fiscal adjustment layer (add-back of non-deductibles per Ley Art. 29-A) + gravable/exenta ledger segregation with 50/50 common-cost allocation (chart-of-accounts design: gravable vs. exenta dimension).
- **Topics:** taxation, fiscal-reporting, chart-of-accounts
- **Doubts/xref:** Mirrors IVA pro-rata problem but with a flat 50/50 rule for common costs (vs. Ley IVA Art. 66 sales-based proration — different tax, different method; do not conflate). Links Ley Art. 29/29-A (deductions/non-deductions, 03_).

## EVID-144 Reglamento ISR — retentions: surviving Art. 59 (part-time = servicio permanente) + repealed machinery

- **Loc:** Art. 59 (p.14); Arts. 57-58, 60-65-A DEROGADO (3) (pp.14-15); retention chapter heading "DE LAS RETENCIONES" (p.14).

CON CARÁCTER DE SUBORDINACIÓN O DEPENDENCIA" — Art. 59 consistent but partially redundant; dependence/subordination qualifier added by the law may narrow Art. 59's broader reading (OQ-5).

## EVID-145 Reglamento ISR — depreciation register (Art. 84 survivor) + repealed books/inventory regime

- **Loc:** Art. 84 (p.16); Arts. 81-83, 85-101 DEROGADO (3) (pp.16-17); chapter heading "DE LA CONTABILIDAD FORMAL, REGISTROS ESPECIALES O INVENTARIOS" (p.16).
- **Verbatim:** "Las depreciaciones de los bienes dedicados a la producción de ingresos computables se anotarán minuciosamente y detalladamente por medio de registros pormenorizados, debiendo contener, por lo menos, la siguiente información: ESPECIFICACION DEL BIEN, VALOR A DEPRECIAR, FECHA EN QUE COMIENZA A USARSE, PERIODO DE VIDA UTIL, MEJORAS, ADICIONES, CUOTA DE DEPRECIACION, SALDO POR DEPRECIAR, RETIRO, ENAJENACION, y todos los datos que la naturaleza del bien exija." (Art. 84, p.16)
- **Gloss:** Mandatory detailed per-asset depreciation register for income-producing assets, with minimum fields: asset specification, depreciable value, in-use start date, useful-life period, improvements, additions, depreciation quota, remaining balance to depreciate, retirement, disposal — plus whatever the asset's nature demands. NOTE: this is the ONLY surviving article of the entire books/records/special-registers/inventories regime (Título VIII Cap. II, Arts. 81-101); no depreciation rate table exists in this Reglamento (rates live in Ley Art. 30, 03_; no tabs printed here despite expectations in the tasking).
- **Candidate CRs:** Odoo fixed-asset register must expose exactly these per-asset fields (incl. improvements/additions tracked per asset, disposal/retirement events) — direct legal basis for the ISR asset model.
- **Topics:** taxation, chart-of-accounts, fiscal-reporting
- **Doubts/xref:** Art. 84 survived the 2001 repeal that killed its neighbors — likely deliberately kept; interaction with CT Arts. 139-143 (books, 05_ EVID-061) to be verified (OQ-6). No revaluation regime and no intangibles-amortization operationalization survive here (Ley Art. 30-A, 03_, governs intangibles alone).

## EVID-146 Reglamento ISR — employer social funds (aportaciones patronales): accounting entry & deductibility limits

- **Loc:** Art. 141 (p.19); INCISO TERCERO SUPRIMIDO (1) (p.19). Arts. 137-140, 142, 143 DEROGADO (3).
