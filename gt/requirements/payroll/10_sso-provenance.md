## 1. Purpose

This final content file of the S-GT3 payroll wave defines two hygiene
layers. First, the **SSO employer duties** of Acuerdo Gubernativo 229-2014,
*Reglamento de Salud y Seguridad Ocupacional* (Occupational Health and
Safety Regulation, 23-Jul-2014; *vigencia* 8-Sep-2015 as reformed by
Ac. Gu. 51-2015/199-2015; consolidated with reforms 33-2016 and 57-2022;
derogating the 28-Dec-1957 higiene reglamento): the *comité bipartito de
Salud y Seguridad Ocupacional* (bipartite SSO committee) at workplaces with
ten or more workers versus the *monitor de salud y seguridad ocupacional*
(SSO monitor) below ten — both registered and authorized by MinTrabajo or
IGSS; the registered SSO plan with its **three-year review** cycle; the
occupational medical-exam ladder (pre-employment etc.) as
onboarding-adjacent tasks; the sanction flow to Código de Trabajo arts.
271-272 (values external); and the grave-danger suspension power of
Art. 552 bis. By R40 the instrument is **money-free**: AG 229-2014 contains
ZERO cuota/recaudación/payroll-money provisions — headcount-triggered TASKS
only, never an IGSS-rate source (negative FR, kin to the GT-PAY-FR-184
guard of file 07).

Second, the **edition-provenance citation rules** for the whole wave,
stated as machine-actionable requirements (cluster P7; the payroll sibling
of `gt/requirements/taxation/07_reform-chain-provenance.md`): the CT
edition qualifier — every CT labor citation the product emits carries
"(texto según edición conmemorativa 2024, MinTrabajo)" (R44; files 01-06
carry it on their LB rows — this file defines the rule); the
no-"current-through" rule with the GOQ-71 per-article currency
re-verification flag (the edition's consolidation cutoff is indeterminate:
latest printed annotation D18-2001 with demonstrably later unannotated
content); the cited-only-if-resolved guard on arts. 420/421 (stacked
*Derogado+Reformado* / double derogations — GOQ-72); the D-1441
instrument-dating acquisition pointer (GOQ-70 — the commemorative book
prints no year and is not a DCA/congressional print); the 34_ reform-date
shuffle flag (GOQ-76) on every SSO dated row; and the CT special-regime
scope-boundary rows (agro, trabajo a domicilio, domésticos, art. 197 BIS,
trabajo de familia art. 199) pointing at the SR-wave territory.

It does **not** cover: salaries, jornadas, bonuses, vacaciones, contracts
(files 01-06); IGSS cuotas, planilla electrónica and rates
(`07_igss-contributions.md`); IRTRA/INTECAP (`08_irtra-intecap.md`);
ISR/IVA payroll interfaces (`09_isr-iva-interfaces.md`); sanction values
(owned by `gt/requirements/taxation/06_ct-procedures.md` via the Código
Tributario art. 94 family — never re-derived here); the nursery duty
substance (owned by `05_vacaciones-maternidad.md` GT-PAY-FR-124 — this
file records the adjacency only); the special-regime substantive rules
(S-GT6/S-GT7 SR-wave); or the taxation-instrument provenance rows

`gt/requirements/catalogs/01_governance.md`.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 SSO employer duties — headcount-triggered tasks (AG 229-2014; money-free by R40)

- **GT-PAY-FR-223:** The system shall model the SSO representation duty as a
  headcount-triggered company obligation: every workplace (*lugar de
  trabajo*) with **ten or more workers** must count with a *comité
  bipartito de Salud y Seguridad Ocupacional* (bipartite SSO committee)
  integrated with an equal number of worker and employer representatives —
  not substitutable by any commission or brigade with similar functions;
  workplaces with **fewer than ten workers** must count with a *monitor de
  salud y seguridad ocupacional* (SSO monitor) in charge of risk-prevention
  management. Committees (with their *libros de actas* (minute books)) and
  monitors shall be registered and authorized — physically or
  electronically — by the Ministerio de Trabajo y Previsión Social or the
  IGSS (IGSS informs MinTrabajo for a unified national registry); the
  system records the registration/authorization data and the committee or
  monitor assignment per workplace. Task surface only — no money line
  (FR-229). (LB-003; EVID-325)
- **GT-PAY-FR-224:** Every workplace shall carry an SSO plan record
  registered with MinTrabajo or the IGSS (where IGSS registers it must
  immediately inform MinTrabajo, for a unified national registry), with a mandatory **review-every-three-years** cycle
  (*revisados cada tres años para que sean actualizados*) surfaced as a
  compliance reminder, plus immediate-update triggers: new work processes,
  enterprise mergers, new technology, infrastructure modifications, or
  personnel increases/decreases that raise or lower occupational risks.
  (LB-004; EVID-325)
- **GT-PAY-FR-225:** The system shall surface the occupational
  medical-exam ladder of Art. 303 as HR tasks: the *examen pre-empleo*
  (pre-employment examination) hooks onboarding, and *vigilancia médica*,
  *vigilancia epidemiológica* and *diagnóstico* (medical surveillance,
  epidemiological surveillance, diagnosis — the *historia clínica
  ocupacional* (occupational clinical history), performable only by
  competent physicians) hook ongoing employment records. Exam scheduling
  metadata only; exam outcomes are medical records, never payroll inputs;
  no money line (FR-229). (LB-005; EVID-325)
- **GT-PAY-FR-226:** SSO enforcement shall be recorded as a POINTER ONLY:
  any violation of any prescriptive or prohibitive provision of
  AG 229-2014, by action or omission, gives rise to a sanction as
  established in **CT arts. 271 and 272** — this file records the routing
  and NO sanction value; the money values are owned by
  `gt/requirements/taxation/06_ct-procedures.md` via the Código Tributario
  art. 94 family cross-ref (filename-only pointer, never re-derived here;
  kin to the GT-PAY-FR-025 enforcement pointers of file 01).
  (LB-006; EVID-325)
- **GT-PAY-FR-227:** The system shall record the grave-danger suspension
  power as a compliance-surface datum: where the gravity or imminence of
  danger warrants, MinTrabajo and the IGSS may suspend all or some premises
  of a workplace and prohibit the use of machines, devices, apparatus or
  equipment offering grave danger to the life, health or bodily integrity
  of the workers, until the necessary safety measures are taken
  (Art. 552 bis). Suspension events halt the affected workplace's
  attendance/jornada records for their duration (kin to the suspension
  surfaces of `07_igss-contributions.md`); no payroll computation derives
  from this file. (LB-006; EVID-325)
- **GT-PAY-FR-228:** The SSO duty register shall record the nursery-duty
  ADJACENCY ONLY: the *guardería* obligation of CT art. 155 (employers
  with more than thirty female workers must fit out a nursing/care room
  for children under three) is owned by
  `05_vacaciones-maternidad.md` **GT-PAY-FR-124** (consumed by id; this
  file never re-derives it) — the SSO surface lists it among the
  MinTrabajo-facing facility duties flagged when the headcount/worker
  composition crosses the trigger. (LB-003; GT-PAY-FR-124 cross-ref;
  EVID-325)
- **GT-PAY-FR-229:** NEGATIVE FR (R40 scope guard): AG 229-2014 is an
  *orden público* occupational-safety instrument containing ZERO
  cuota/recaudación/payroll-money provisions — NO payroll money
  computation, IGSS rate, cuota, retención, mora or planilla mechanic may
  cite AG 229-2014 (34_) as source; SSO content yields headcount-triggered
  TASKS only (FR-223..228). Kin guard already rides the shared
  citation-hygiene catalog via `07_igss-contributions.md`
  **GT-PAY-FR-184** (AG 229-2014 never a rate source — R40); this FR owns
  the payroll-side complement. (LB-001; LB-006; EVID-324; R40)

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given a workplace whose headcount crosses from 8 to 10
  workers, when the SSO representation recomputes, then the mode switches
  from monitor to comité bipartito with the parity-representation and
  MINTRAB/IGSS registration requirements flagged; given a 9-worker
  workplace, then mode = monitor with registered actas book and
  risk-prevention plan. (FR-223)
- **AC-002:** Given an SSO plan registered with IGSS on 1-Mar-2026, when
  the review cycle computes, then review_due = 1-Mar-2029 (3-year cycle)
  and the immediate-update trigger fires when a new work process is
  recorded mid-cycle. (FR-224)
- **AC-003:** Given a new hire entering onboarding, when the SSO task
  engine runs, then the examen pre-empleo task is raised (historia clínica
  ocupacional ladder), and no payroll line is ever generated from the exam
  record. (FR-225)
- **AC-004:** Given a recorded SSO violation, when the enforcement surface
  renders, then it cites CT arts. 271 y 272 with zero monetary sanction
  value — the money values resolve only via the taxation/06 cross-ref
  (Código Tributario art. 94 family). (FR-226)
- **AC-005:** Given a grave/imminent-danger suspension recorded by
  MinTrabajo/IGSS against a workplace's premises and equipment, when the
  compliance surface renders, then the suspension carries authority, scope
  (premises/equipment), dates and the until-safety-measures condition of
  Art. 552 bis. (FR-227)
- **AC-006:** Given an employer with 35 female workers, when the SSO duty
  register renders, then the nursery adjacency row appears pointing at
  GT-PAY-FR-124 (file 05) — and this file contributes no nursery
  requirement text of its own. (FR-228)
- **AC-007:** Given any attempt to seed an IGSS rate, cuota, retención,
  mora or planilla mechanic citing AG 229-2014 (34_), when validated, then
  it is rejected (R40 money-free guard; kin GT-PAY-FR-184) — the SSO
  register holds task surfaces only and contains zero monetary fields.
  (FR-229)
- **AC-008:** Given any CT citation the product emits (requirement row,
  seeded config, diagnostic string), when inspected, then it carries
  "(texto según edición conmemorativa 2024, MinTrabajo)"; given a bare CT
  citation, then the CI gate fails naming FR-230. (FR-230, FR-236)
- **AC-009:** Given any "current through" / *vigente hasta* /
  *consolidado al* claim bound to the 32_ edition, when validated, then it
  is rejected; given a CT-sourced implementable row, then it carries the
  per-article currency re-verification flag with status open.
  (FR-231, FR-236)
- **AC-010:** Given a requirement citing CT art. 420 or art. 421 as live,
  derogated or reformed text, when validated, then it fails unless a
  resolution record against an official instrument exists (stacked
  *Derogado+Reformado / double derogations unresolved). (FR-232, FR-236)
- **AC-011:** Given the 32_ provenance row, when inspected, then
  given/sanctioned/published/effective are all null, the Oct-2024 string
  is stored as print provenance only, and the GOQ-70 acquisition pointer
  is set; given the 34_ row, then it carries the shuffle flag with dates
  from the outer title pairing only (23-Jul-2014; 33-2016 05-Feb-2016;
  57-2022 01-Mar-2022; vigencia 8-Sep-2015) and the inner-title mis-
  pairing sits verbatim [sic] in the defect ledger. (FR-233, FR-234)
- **AC-012:** Given the scope register, when read, then it contains
  exactly the five CT special-regime boundary rows (agro 139-146;
  domicilio 156-160 with the 2× sanction of art. 159; domésticos 161-163;
  art. 197 BIS; trabajo de familia art. 199), each a one-liner pointer to
  the SR-wave, none elaborated into a requirement of this wave.
  (FR-235)
- **AC-013:** Given the Odoo Mapping table, when checked, then every FR
  row carries a Layer value: SSO duty surfaces = `odoo`; sanction pointer,
  guards and provenance register rows = `shared`; the CI validation gate =
  `saas`. (FR-223..236)
