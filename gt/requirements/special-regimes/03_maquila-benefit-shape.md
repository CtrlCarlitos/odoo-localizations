## 1. Purpose

This file is the anchor of the GT special-regimes wave's maquila (D-29-89)
BENEFIT SHAPE — the wave's most myth-infested topic, anchored on the negative
finding R75: under the law as consolidated through D-19-2016, the classic
maquiladora/exportadora bajo el Régimen de Admisión Temporal (temporary
admission regime, AT) is an ORDINARY ISR/IVA taxpayer whose only law-level
benefit is the ONE-YEAR suspensión of derechos arancelarios e impuestos a la
importación incl. IVA of art. 12, on inputs listed in the calificación
(qualification) resolution, per accepted import declaration — **"maquila =
ISR-exempt" is a rejected myth**, and this file leads with that guard. On
that base it fixes: the art. 12 bis bundle for the two D-19-2016 categories
only — productora AT (vestuario/textiles, SA sección XI capítulos 50-63) and
prestadora de servicios (ICT-linked services to non-residents) — whose
centerpiece is the art. 12 bis d) "Exoneración total" of ISR for DIEZ (10)
AÑOS counted per-beneficiary from notificación of the calificación
resolution, with the activity-purity scope, the contabilidad de costos +
inventarios perpetuos conditions (cross-referenced to the chart-of-accounts
books anchors by exact id), the costos-unitarios fallback and the
foreign-branch carve-out; the companion 12 bis benefits (machinery/equipment
exoneración total (c) OR the alternative 1-year suspensión (e); the
fuel-oil/butane/propane/bunker exoneration for electric self-generation (f);
local inputs/services outside IVA via constancias (g) — pointer-only to the
fiscal-reporting constancia surfaces); the seven calificación categories and
the dual-calificación rule (allowed EXCEPT productora AT × prestadora, no
benefit duplication); the four perfeccionamiento regimes (AT / Devolución de
Derechos / Reposición con Franquicia Arancelaria / CANT) with the

guard; the DEROGATED art. 15 (2005-state recovered from `94_` as the CANT
benefits article — incisos b) ISR + d) fuel; the AT holiday lived in
