## 1. Purpose

This file is the GT special-regimes wave's CROSS-REGIME synthesis (cluster
SR6) — the bridge layer between the two sibling chains: the maquila chain
(D-29-89 / AG 533-89 / AG 253-2001) and the zonas francas chain (D-65-89 /
AG 242-90 / AG 296-94 / D-6-2021 / AG 65-2022). It owns every surface where
one chain's printed text reaches into the other: (i) the **exclusion
matrix** — a firm revocation of benefits under any of D-29-89, D-65-89 or
D-22-73 (Ley Orgánica de la Zona Libre de Industria y Comercio Santo Tomás
de Castilla, ZOLIC — outside corpus) disqualifies NEW qualification in BOTH
chains (maquila law art. 4 bis; ZF law art. 5 Bis; the maquila reglamento
art. 9 j) no-revocation sworn declaration spanning the three laws), with

**inter-regime merchandise flows** — D-29-89 Admisión Temporal enterprises
and ZF usuarios may send/receive merchandise for transformation,
elaboration or product completion outside IVA, with a duty guarantee on
ZF-outbound legs (art. 36 bis), the coexportación mechanism (art. 36 ter)
and the CAUCA-documented declaración de mercancías + prior-notification
requirement (reglamento arts. 42 bis/42 ter); (iii) the **textiles
national-sales valve** (art. 39 bis, added by D-19-2016): vestuario and
textiles industrial goods destined to the national customs territory may

is not IVA-affected, and the ZF side's national-territory sales income is

art. 35 bis deferring the D-29-89 art. 39 bis payment procedure to SAT;

already enjoyed under DL 21-84 / D-29-89 are deducted from the new

computation over the window-row models of files 01/03; and (vi) THE GOQ-08
