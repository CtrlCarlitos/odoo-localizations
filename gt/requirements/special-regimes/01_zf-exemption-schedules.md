## 1. Purpose

This file is the anchor of the GT special-regimes wave's ZF (zonas francas,
free zones) benefit matrix: it converts the exemption schedules of the *Ley de
Zonas Francas* (ZF Law, Decreto 65-89) art. 21/22, the reglamento (AG 242-90)
ISR/IVA mechanics, the fondo (specific fund) instruments (AG 296-94) and the
MINECO tarifario (fee schedule, AG 65-2022) into requirements as **per-
beneficiary D15 dated rows** — starting from the load-bearing anchor: every
exemption window is a dated row on the beneficiary keyed by a qualification
event (notificación of the calificación resolution / art. 7 b) operation
authorization / período-de-imposición start / resolution-granted plazo),
**never a global constant**. On that base it fixes: the administradora
(administrator entity) ISR "Exoneración total" 15 años window anchored to the
inicio del período de imposición inmediato siguiente to the art. 7 b)
operation authorization (with the exterior-domicile carve-out and the D-25-91
"artículo 7*" citation artifact); the usuario (user) ISR "Exoneración total"
10 años window anchored to notificación of the calificación resolution (with
the post-31-03-2016 two-class user taxonomy of art. 4 — productores de bienes
industriales + servicios — and its TAN-facing/exterior carve-outs); the
GOQ-08 verdict on the post-2016 window shape, now RESOLVED from primary
(D-19-2016 = `84_`: art. 22 b) KEEPS 10 años from notificación — no
nominal change; the reform's substance = the art. 4 taxonomy + the
TAN-sales carve-out + the old-category transition; surviving gaps = the
pre-2016 art. 22 wording and AG 52-2017-dependent reglamento content —
EVID-755/757/787);
the inmuebles (real-property) 5-year exoneration on the gobierno-central
share ONLY with its trimestre-anchored clock; the purpose-bound
alcabala/timbres family; the 100% resolution-identified import and fuel
exonerations; the IVA geometry (intra/inter-ZF transfers no afectas; local
inputs + exclusive-use services no afectas via definitive-export treatment by
the local producer, consumed alongside the taxation/03 exporter-65% rows by
exact id); the art. 25 TAN-sales channel (≤20% de producción total, per-sale
floor US$5,000, single consignatario) with the art. 18 ISR prorrata; the ISR
determination valve (full LAT Libro I computation consigned exonerado,
consumed into the taxation/05 Utilidades engine by exact id); the fondo
liability (US$0.10/m²/month on área arrendada/ocupada/vendida, first-5-días
deadline, 5+3+5 liquidation chain at the prior-day USD rate, base exclusions,
fraction-of-month = full month); the MINECO tarifario in USD at the Banguat
daily reference rate (the TWO FX anchors kept as distinct rules, GOQ-139);
the transition rows (D-6-2021 transitorio grandfathering per own-resolution
plazo incl. ampliación de incisos arancelarios; the historical art. 53
year-deduction); the instrument dated-identity rows (DCA+30 with DCA
unprinted — GOQ-135; AG 296-94 defects — GOQ-138; AG 65-2022 split vigencia;
D-6-2021 publication-day effectiveness); and the negative anchors from the
evidence synthesis (no investment/employment thresholds, no bracket tables,
no 12-year window in this chain).

It does **not** cover: the ZF instrument-chain identity and consolidation
state (D-65-89 → AG 242-90 → AG 296-94 → D-6-2021 → AG 65-2022, the 68_
mixed-consolidation ledger, the D-19-2016 gap map) — `02_zf-chain-regime.md`

cross-regime bridges (`06_cross-regime-bridges.md` SR6); the qualification

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001:** Given any ZF beneficiary configuration, when its exemption
  windows are inspected, then every window is a dated row on the
  beneficiary keyed by a qualification event with computed valid_from — and
  no global/window constant artifact exists anywhere in the configuration.
  (FR-001, FR-002)
- **AC-002:** Given an administradora whose art. 7 b) operation
  authorization event is dated mid-year, when its ISR window row is
  computed, then valid_from = the start of the ISR período de imposición
  immediately following that authorization and valid_to = 15 años later,
  with the "artículo 7*" provenance note and the exterior carve-out flags
  present. (FR-003, FR-004, FR-005)
- **AC-003:** Given a usuario whose calificación resolution was notified on
  date D, when its ISR window row is computed, then valid_from = D and
  valid_to = 10 años later; and given a pre-D-19-2016 grandfathered user
  with a resolution-granted plazo, then the resolution plazo governs
  (window_source = resolution) including ampliación de incisos
  arancelarios. (FR-006, FR-029)
- **AC-004:** Given a usuario qualifying after 31-03-2016, when its ISR
  window row is saved, then it carries the d19_2016_primary provenance
  (`84_` art. 23: 10 años from notificación) and any post-2016
  window-shape assertion beyond the primary text (e.g. a nominal duration
  change, or a pre-2016 12/15-year wording) is refused.
  (FR-009)
- **AC-005:** Given a ZF property within its 5-year inmuebles window, when
  the property-tax computation runs, then only the gobierno-central share
  is exonerated (the municipal share computes fully) and the window is
  trimestre-anchored with the anti-stretch hard stop. (FR-010, FR-011)
- **AC-006:** Given a merchandise transfer between two ZF users (same or
  different zones), when the move posts, then it is IVA no afecta; and
  given a user's purchase of local production inputs for final-product
  incorporation, then the purchase is documented under the producer-side
  definitive-export treatment consumed with GT-TAX-FR-072/073.
  (FR-017, FR-018)
- **AC-007:** Given an industrial user's TAN sale, when validation runs,
  then it passes only with a DPI authorization, cumulative sales ≤ 20% de
  producción total, per-sale amount ≥ US$5,000 national-currency
  equivalent and a single consignatario; and the annual return carries the
  prorrata computation + authorization copies, computing exonerated ISR =
  total ISR × (non-TAN sales ÷ renta bruta both sources) with no reference
  to the derogated art. 26. (FR-019, FR-020)
- **AC-008:** Given a ZF beneficiary inside its ISR window, when the annual
  ISR return is prepared, then the tax is fully determined under LAT Libro
  I (Utilidades engine by GT-TAX-FR-152/153) and consigned as exonerado
  citing arts. 21/22 b). (FR-021)
- **AC-009:** Given an administradora's fondo month with 10,000 m² leased,
  2,000 m² occupied rent-free and 500 m² of own offices plus the aduanera
  delegation excluded, when the declaration computes, then the base =
  12,000 m² × US$0.10 at the prior-day USD rate, a 10-day partial
  occupancy counts as a full month, and the 5+3+5 deadline chain is
  tracked with receipt evidence. (FR-022, FR-023, FR-024)
- **AC-010:** Given the two conversion surfaces, when the fondo liquidation
  and a tarifario fee resolve, then the fondo uses the prior-day USD rate
  rule (source-unspecified flag) and the tarifario uses the Banguat daily
  reference rule — two distinct dated rows, and any configuration that
  points both at one rule is refused. (FR-024, FR-026, FR-027, FR-028)
- **AC-011:** Given the seeded MINECO tarifario, when listed, then it holds
  the 9 USD items with item 9 = "Cancelación de resolución de autorización
  $500.00" per 81_ (never 68_'s duplicated "Adición de incisos
  arancelarios" text) and valid_from 2022-03-15. (FR-026)
- **AC-012:** Given any ZF configuration scan, when negative guards run,
  then no investment/employment threshold, bracket table, 12-year window or
  municipal-share suppression exists anywhere. (FR-032, FR-033)
- **AC-013:** Given the instrument identity rows, when inspected, then
  D-65-89 stores vigencia = DCA + 30 días with the DCA-unknown flag (never
  an invented date), AG 296-94 stores next-day vigencia with the "83"→183
  garble note, AG 65-2022 stores the 15-Mar/14-Sep-2022 split, and D-6-2021
  stores 2021-06-01 publication-day effectiveness (never +90 days).
