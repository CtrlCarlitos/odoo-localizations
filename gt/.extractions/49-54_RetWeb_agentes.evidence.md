## Open questions (file-level, numbered OQ-1..)

- **OQ-1:** `49_`/`50_` are Wayback captures (2025-06-01 per registry) — the pages themselves print only "© … 2025"; `49_`'s in-page link "Listado Agentes de Retención al 01/06/2025" anchors its content to ≥ 01-Jun-2025; `50_` has no internal date anchor at all.
- **OQ-2:** The 5% rates (Pequeño Contribuyente ≥ Q2,500.01; Agropecuario on total factura) and the "valor total" qualifier for the 1.5% fuel rate are printed by `49_`/`52_` but absent from the W-GT2 rate list as handed down. Treated as **additions**, not contradictions; controller to reconcile against the D27-92 art. 54-bis / D-20-2006 text (not in this corpus) before freezing the rate matrix.
- **OQ-3:** `49_` does not restate the even-if-zero IVA filing duty (D-20-2006 art. 12 kin per plan); and it splits the IVA deadline by regime — **SAT-2340 15 días hábiles vs SAT-2320 (agro) 10 días hábiles**. The generic "IVA = 15 días" requirement must carry the agro exception. `52_` (manual users) declares via Sistemas de Contabilidad del Estado, outside the manual's scope.
- **OQ-4:** Version/date identity of the two manuals rests on labels + the portal (`49_` shows "Usuarios Manuales … 29-11-2024"); `51_` prints "noviembre 2024" but **not** "v3"; `52_` prints **no date**. `54_` prints no form version/date either.
- **OQ-5:** `53_` OCR defects: 25 of 8,447 rows have NIT/name/date concatenated (e.g. rows 8379, 8405); roster carries no totals/notes; agent count derived from the running No. (8,447) — verify against a fresh download before seeding master data.
- **OQ-6:** Deadline qualifiers as printed: `50_` constancia delivery = "primeros (5) días" **without** "hábiles" (the 10-day ISR rule says "hábiles"); IVA constancia date = agent-chosen "Emisión al" date (`52_`) vs ISR constancia date = invoice date (`50_`) — intentional asymmetry, keep both rules; the legal qualifier for the 5-day term needs D-10-2012 (outside corpus).
- **OQ-7:** No DTE type code for constancias (no "RET"/"CIVA"-family strings) appears anywhere in this corpus; `51_` shows constancias generated/downloaded inside Retenciones Web (PDF), and FEL invoices carry the legends "Sujeto a retención definitiva" / "Sujeto a pagos trimestrales ISR". Whether the constancia is itself a FEL DTE family member must come from W-GT1 evidence.
- **OQ-8:** `52_` cites "artículo 54 B del Decreto Número 27-92" (printed "54 B"); consolidated D27-92 nomenclature is "54 BIS" — equating them requires the D27-92 text (W-GT2). Also `51_` p.9's terse "7% + excedente de Q1,500.00" for the over-Q30,000 OS bracket is printed as-is; the law's exact wording (5% first Q30,000 + 7% of excess) is outside this corpus.
- **OQ-9:** `53_` has no agent-type/category column — the roster cannot seed exportador/especial/público/otros distinctions; and it mixes private and (extensive) public-sector entities in one running number. The agent-type attribute must come from another source (RTU / SAT calificación), not this roster.
