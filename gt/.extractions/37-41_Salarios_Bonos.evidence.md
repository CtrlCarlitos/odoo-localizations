**Identity verdicts (checked first, from text):**

COVID freeze agreement — 2021 rates = 2020 rates, single national zone. File ALSO carries a second, unrelated instrument from p.3 (MinFin AG 243-2020, INFOM budget decrease) — see EVID-338.
- `39_` = CONFIRMED **official history compilation 1995–2021** ("SALARIOS MÍNIMOS FIJADOS POR EL GOBIERNO DE GUATEMALA PARA LOS AÑOS COMPRENDIDOS DE 1,995 A 2,021." / "Recopilación: Mario R. de Mata Guerra. NOT itself a legal instrument — an authority-compiled secondary table citing each year's AG with emis/publication dates and vigencia ranges.
- `40_` = CONFIRMED **Decreto 42-92** ("DECRETO No. 42-92", given 2-Jul-1992; compendium footnote "* Publicado en el Diario Oficial el 2 de julio de 1992") = **Ley de Bonificación Anual para Trabajadores del Sector Privado y Público** — i.e. the **"BONO 14"** law (paid in July; `38_`'s budget print carries the line "Bonificación anual (Bono 14)"). The FILE NAME "Aguinaldo" is WRONG: art. 1 expressly calls the prestación "adicional e independiente al aguinaldo anual".
- `41_` = CONFIRMED **Decreto 78-89** ("DECRETO NUMERO 78-89", dated December 1989, day OCR-illegible) = **bonificación incentivo** law (hourly floor Q0.15/Q0.30). The FILE NAME "Bono14" is WRONG: this decree creates the bonificación incentivo, not bono 14 — the bono-14 decree is D42-92, sitting in the file mislabeled "Aguinaldo". The two file names are effectively swapped.

**Terminology corrections (project-level):** (i) "Bono 14" = bonificación anual (July window) created by **D42-92** — NOT D78-89; (ii) D78-89 created the **bonificación incentivo**, whose successor **D37-2001** fixes the flat Q250.00/month (quoted in `39_`, EVID-344) — the Q250 is the *incentivo*, not bono 14 and not a fixed Q3,000/year, and bono 14 is neither a fixed amount nor one minimum wage (EVID-346); (iii) in `37_`, "CE1"/"CE2" are **Circunscripciones Económicas** (territorial: CE1 = departamento de Guatemala; CE2 = all other departments), crossed with 3 activities (Agrícola / No Agrícola / Exportadora y de Maquila) — not "actividad/actividad mixta"; (iv) the December aguinaldo statutes (D76-78 private, D1633 public) are only cross-referenced in `40_` — their texts are NOT in this corpus (OQ-10).
