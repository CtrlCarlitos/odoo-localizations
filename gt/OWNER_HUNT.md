### 1a. IGSS SEM/accidentes rate instruments — the Acuerdo 410 art.-85 chain

Acuerdos de la **Junta Directiva del IGSS** extending/reforming Acuerdo 410
(Reglamento del Seguro de Enfermedad y Maternidad, 1967):

**475 → 616 → 621 → 849 → 1024 → 1095 → 1121 → 1243**

- Search: `acuerdo` + the number, filtered to IGSS / Junta Directiva,
  1960s–1970s decades.
- CAUTION: an IGSS "Acuerdo 1243" from **2010** exists (SEM extension to
  Santa Rosa/El Progreso/Petén) — that is the WRONG family; we want the
  1970s chain member. Check the date before celebrating.
- Why: the operative SEM/accidentes cuota percentages. Everything printed
  in corpus (1002 art. 46's 4%/2%/1½%, 410 art. 62's 4/2/1 vs 3/2/1) is
  superseded-era text — never citable for a rate.

### 1b. The "normativa institucional" fixing the contribución-mínima percentages

- The instrument that Acuerdo 1421 **art. 5** (as reformed by Acuerdo 1520,
  2022) delegates the percentages to ("normativa institucional vigente").
- Search: IGSS/Junta Directiva acuerdos touching **contribución mínima /
  porcentajes / cuotas**; also try `Instituto Guatemalteco de Seguridad
  Social porcentajes` on legal.dca full text.
- Why: the EMA 4/2 + Accidentes 3/1 rate source. 1520's distribution
  proportions are corroboration-only — never a rate source.
